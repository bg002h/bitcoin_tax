### C‑1 — Critical — R3's premise is false; `extension_payment` already exists, is collected, and already prints

**Where:** spec R3 (`:78-84`) and T3 (`:120-122`).
**What is wrong:** R3 says "`ReturnInputs` gains `extension_payment: Option<Usd>`" and T3 says "first
measures Schedule 3 line 10's provenance today; **if it is a structural blank**, it becomes this
collected value." It is not a structural blank. `Payments.extension_payment: Usd`
(`return_inputs.rs:708`) is collected by the TUI (`sections.rs:1043`) and by `income import`
(`fullreturn_inputs.toml:97`), enters the exact `total_payments` (`return_1040.rs:2338`), reaches
`PrintedInputs` (`:2451`), and prints on Schedule 3 line 10 (`printed.rs:1543`) with three tests
holding it. Built as written — a *new* field on `ReturnInputs` wired to line 10 while
`payments.extension_payment` keeps feeding the exact chain — the printed and exact chains diverge on
the return's bottom line, which is the two-chain wrong-figure class this repo has already been bitten
by. R2's second refusal is specified against the new field and would key on whichever one the
implementer picked. The spec also had a one-command measurement available and deferred it to a task.
**Minimal change:** rewrite R3 to state the measured fact — the field exists at
`payments.extension_payment`, collected on both surfaces, printing on Schedule 3 line 10 — and reduce
the change to the single open question: whether it becomes `Option<Usd>` so a $0 line 10 on a filed
Schedule 3 is a blank rather than a sworn zero. If yes, name the ~10 call sites (`return_1040.rs:2338`,
`:2451`, `printed.rs:1543`, `classifier.rs:782`, `return_refuse.rs:815`, `sections.rs:1043-1048`,
`coverage.rs:611`, `scrub_axis.rs:344`, `testonly.rs:361`, the two fixtures) as the blast radius. Drop
T3's "if it is a structural blank" branch.

### C‑2 — Critical — neither new emitter declares the pseudo-reconciled attestation gate or the DRAFT watermark

**Where:** R2's refusal list (`:68-76`), R4 (`:86-91`), T4/T5.
**What is wrong:** every existing path that emits a filled IRS form gates on `state.pseudo_active()`:
`require_attestation(attest)?` then `stamp_draft_watermark` on every page (`admin.rs:624-630`,
`:1022-1025`, `:1094-1099`), and `cli.rs:199-200` states it as a product guarantee — *"PSEUDO-RECONCILED
ledgers: the same attestation gate as export-snapshot applies, AND every page is stamped with a
diagonal `DRAFT — ESTIMATE, NOT FOR FILING` watermark."* R2 lists three refusals and one warning, none
of them this; R4 says only "beside the packet". A pseudo-active ledger would therefore produce a
**clean, unwatermarked Form 4868 carrying a dollar figure the filer mails a cheque against** — worse
than the DRAFT return it was derived from, because the 4868 and the 1040-V are the two forms money is
physically attached to. Note also the repo's own precedent that a `.txt` riding with DRAFT forms must
say so; a payment voucher is the same shape.
**Minimal change:** add to R2 and R4: when `state.pseudo_active()`, `require_attestation` first (no
bytes on refusal) and `stamp_draft_watermark` every page of `f4868.pdf` / `f1040v.pdf`; add the kills
to T4/T5 (pseudo + no attest ⇒ refusal, zero bytes; pseudo + attest ⇒ watermarked bytes). State
explicitly that `promote_export_gate` is **not** required (the 4868 discloses no position), so the
omission is a decision rather than a gap.

### I‑1 — Important — MFS prints a spouse SSN and spouse names on boxes the form conditions on a JOINT return

**Where:** 4868 line 3 row (`:29`), 1040-V box 2 (`:43`) and `f1_7`/`f1_8` (`:46`).

### I‑6 — Important — the "already recorded" refusal blocks the natural journey and misses the zero-payment case

**Where:** R2 (`:71-72`) and the journey row (`:105`).
**What is wrong:** `extension_payment` records **a payment**, not **that a Form 4868 was filed**, so
the refusal is keyed to the wrong fact in both directions. It **over-fires** on a plausible and
invited order: the input form has a `PayExtension` field, so a filer who decides to pay $2,000 with
their extension records it, then runs `tax extension` — and is refused *on the first run*, told a
form is "filed once" when they have filed nothing. Their only remedy is to un-record a true fact. It
**under-fires** on a $0-payment extension, which the form expressly permits (*"If you find you can't
pay the amount shown on line 6, you can still get the extension"*): that filer is never "recorded", so
the guard never applies to them. It also blocks a legitimate reprint after mailing.
**Minimal change:** invert it — a recorded `extension_payment` becomes the **default for `--pay`**,

### I‑7 — Important — `--pay-by-check` on a crypto-slice year is unspecified and would be silently ignored

**Where:** R4 (`:86-91`).
**What is wrong:** `--pay-by-check` lands on `ExportIrsPdf`, whose two pipelines are dispatched inside

**Minimal change:** refuse `--pay-by-check` on a year with no full-return inputs, naming the reason

### I‑8 — Important — no bundled year can drive either filler to success, so their success paths have no reachable end-to-end kill
