Two independent checks, neither of which reads a rendered page:

**(a) Gutter-label join.** For each mapped field, its `/Rect` was flipped into `pdftotext -bbox`

Line 22's six cells carry no gutter label, so they were checked structurally instead: x-band against
the printed column headers — `(i)` at x=182.7 over the [108,380] comb, `(ii)` at x=439.3 over
[432,503.2], `(iii)` at x=509.2 over [504,576] — and row letter against the margin `a` (ytop 161.7) /
`b` (179.7). All six matched.

**(b) Quote check.** Every double-quoted span inside the map's comments (56 of them) was normalized
(dot leaders stripped, whitespace collapsed) and searched in `design/forms/extract/f1040s1a--2025.txt`:
**53 found verbatim.** The 3 that are not are all accounted for: two are repo prose, not form text
(*"every field is accounted for"*, *"we forgot this line"*), and the third is the line-22 column (ii)
header *"Deducted on Schedule C, Schedule E, or Schedule F"*, which is on the page but which
`pdftotext -layout` transposes — the longest contiguous run it preserves is `"(ii) Deducted on"`,
verified present. The map states that truncation where it quotes the header, matching the note already

**(c) Render (belt and braces).** `xtask label-proof f1040s1a--2025` fills every box through the

rendered and read. Every box shows the line printed beside it; 36a/36b/37/38 — the four whose rows are
closest together — land correctly. The VIN comb visibly renders **one character per cell**, which is
the map's reason for requiring bare 17-character input.

## 5. What changed vs TY2024

**Nothing was ported, because nothing exists to port.** Schedule 1-A was created by Pub. L. 119-21 for
TY2025: `crates/btctax-forms/forms/2024/` holds no `f1040s1a.map.toml` and `design/forms/extract/` holds
no `f1040s1a--2024.txt`. Every field name here was read from this year's blank; every line's text from
this year's committed text layer. The TY2024-port hazards catalogued in
`design/ty2025/recon-year-port-delta.md` (renamed fields, a changed root subform, a line whose meaning
moved under a stable number) are therefore all vacuous for this form.

Facts about the form worth carrying, all recorded in the map's header:

- **Lines 11, 19 and 28 are STEPS, not money** — whole-number quotients of *"Divide line N by $1,000"*.
  Key names carry `_steps` so an emitter cannot silently format them as dollars.
- **…and they do not round the same way.** 11 and 19 *decrease* to the next lower whole number; **28
  *increases* to the next higher one.** A shared helper that floors all three inflates the car-loan
  deduction — the understatement-of-tax direction.
- **The blanks cut both ways on this schedule.** A wrongly-blank *deduction* line (4a–6, 14a–14c, 22
  col (iii), 23) forgoes a benefit and overstates tax; but a wrongly-blank *MAGI* line (1, 2a–2e, 3, 8,
  16, 25, 31) or *phase-out* line (10–12, 18–20, 27–29, 33–34) shrinks the phase-out and **inflates the
  deduction — an understatement.** This is the substantive argument for mapping every cell.
- **Line 33 jumps with a NONZERO constant**: *"If zero or less, enter $6,000 on line 35"* — not "-0-".
- **Line 22's arity is two rows + a refusal**, decided in `btctax-core::Line22Row`, not here. This map
  is the form's own arity and must never grow a row 22c the PDF does not have.
- **Seams**: line 1 ← 1040 **line 11b**; line 38 → 1040/1040-SR **line 13b** or 1040-NR **line 13c**;
  and it is **line 37**, the senior subtotal — not line 38 — that reaches Form 6251 line 1a.

   the existence of `crates/btctax-forms/forms/{year}/{form}.map.toml` is what means *"btctax emits this
   form"*. `f1040s1a--2025.txt` exists, so quotes for this form are verifiable rather than counted
   `unverifiable` — but any `line_coverage::all()` row naming form `f1040s1a` in 2025 is now checked
   against the extract instead of skipped.
3. **No cargo command was run** (shared worktree, shared lock, per the brief). Every check above came
   from the prebuilt `./target/debug/xtask` binary plus Python over the committed fixtures. The
   workspace test suite — in particular a `map_2025_matches_bundled_pdf_fieldset`-style assertion once
   the struct lands — has not been executed against these two files.
