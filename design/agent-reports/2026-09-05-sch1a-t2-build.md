### The struct

One field per printed line label, in the form's own numbering, each carrying the line's own
instruction text **verbatim** as its doc comment, transcribed from the text layer. Every money leaf is
`Option<Usd>`, so *not completed* is expressible and no line is forced to swear a `0`. Lines 11, 19
and 28 are `line11_steps`/`line19_steps`/`line28_steps` — whole-number quotients, not dollars,
following `qbi_a::line24_ratio`, the in-repo precedent for a non-money printed line.

**Two T2-owned decisions the plan required and did not make:**

* **Line 22's arity: two rows, and a third vehicle is a REFUSAL.** The form prints rows a and b and
  says *"If more than two VINs, see instructions"*; the instructions say to attach a statement, which
  btctax does not emit. Capping silently at two drops a vehicle's interest; summing three into two
  rows gives a correct line 23 while omitting a VIN the deduction is conditioned on. Neither is
  lawful, so the structure is the form's own arity and the refusal is recorded in the type's doc for
  **T3**, where the input surface lands. Pinned so B4 cannot rediscover it.
* **Part I's predicate is the instructions' SECOND sentence.** The first states the condition as a
  disjunction which, read literally, is satisfied by a filer who has excluded Puerto Rico income but
  files no Form 2555 — the case the second sentence sends to lines 2a-2e. The affirmative statement
  governs, and it is the fail-closed direction. Adjudicated in the doc comment rather than left for a
  reviewer to re-derive.

## Where I think the plan is wrong, or was silent

Nothing in the plan was found **wrong**. Three places were **silent** and the build had to decide;
each is recorded above and in the code, and each is a T2-owned decision by the plan's own reasoning:

1. **Line 22's arity** — the plan says *"decide now … and pin the choice with a KAT"* without deciding.
   Decided: two rows, refusal above two, owned by T3.
2. **`printed_line` could not serve 12 of the 48 labels.** CORRECTION 2 orders half 2 to call it; as
   committed it found only bare-numbered labels. Half 2 could not have been built without rewriting
   it. This is not a plan defect — it is work the plan did not know about — but an implementer reading
   *"use `printed_line(<the field's label>)`"* would expect it to work as-is.
3. **Line 22's column headers cannot be quoted contiguously**, because the text layer transposes the
   header block. The doc comment for column (ii) therefore carries the fragment the layer prints, with
   the full printed header stated beside it and the mechanism named.

One thing the plan does **not** say and I chose: `cover_schedule1a` is **registered in `all()`**, so
`xtask line-coverage` checks all 50 quotes and both ratchets move in a diff. Item 8's own premise —
*"T2 is where the first TY2025 rows exist"* — requires it, and it is consistent with item 7: rule (4b)
still cannot **force** this form into the table, so the KAT remains the only thing that can notice a
line missing from it.
