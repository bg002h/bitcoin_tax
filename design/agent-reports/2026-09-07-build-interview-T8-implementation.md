## 2. The TY2025 dependents grid, MAPPED

`crates/btctax-forms/forms/2025/f1040.map.toml` — a `[dependents_grid]` section, **generated by a new
`cargo run -p xtask -- dependents-grid f1040--2025`**, never typed.

### Why not the label reader

`cargo run -p xtask -- label-boxes f1040--2025` reports **`?` — BOX-WITH-NO-LABEL — for all 41 grid
boxes.** `label_reader::witness_text`'s label tokens are bare numerals (`is_numeric_label`), and this
grid numbers its rows `(1)`…`(7)` **in parentheses**. That is a real finding about the shared reader.
Widening `is_numeric_label` would re-key every other form's census on a token class it has never seen,
so the grid's addressing is derived in a new module, `crates/xtask/src/dependents_grid.rs`, from the
same two witnesses the label reader uses (the text layer's tokens; AcroForm geometry, y-flipped),
with the parenthesised label column derived by the same *"the column that accounts for the most
printed lines"* discriminator. Recorded in that module's header.

- The **address** of every cell is derived from geometry. The **meaning** of each slot is a
  transcription, and `SLOT_CAPTIONS` is checked verbatim against the extract (rows (6) and (7) against
  `i1040gi--2025.txt`, because `pdftotext -layout` interleaves those captions across four columns and
  no complete phrase survives contiguously in the form's own text layer — stated in the table).
- The **on-state** is read from the PDF's `/AP /N` dictionary, not typed. Row (7)'s two positions are
  two widgets of ONE field (`c1_28[0]` on `/1`, `c1_28[1]` on `/2`) — the form's own way of saying a
  dependent takes at most one credit. A copied `on = "1"` on the second reads back as set and renders
  blank; the gate asserts the on-state too.

### A statutory correction found while sourcing those figures

My first comments said the TCJA $2,000 was in force *"through TY2025"* and that OBBBA raised it from
TY2026. **Wrong.** Checked against the repo's own archive: Pub. L. 119-21 §70104(a)(2) strikes
`$2,000` and inserts `$2,200`, and §70104(f) applies the amendments *"to taxable years beginning after
December 31, 2024"*
(`legal/text/statute-irc/PLAW-119publ21_OBBBA.txt:5042-5051`, `:5110-5111`) — so **TY2025 is already
$2,200**. Rev. Proc. 2025-32 .03 says it in words, and .05(1) republishes $2,200 for TY2026
(`legal/text/irs-guidance/RevProc_2025-32.txt:180-186`, `:590-594`). §24(h)(4)'s $500 is amended by
neither and is not indexed. Every comment now says TY2024, and the pin below is keyed to it.

`advisories.rs`'s `ctc_provably_zero` multiplied by a bare `2000`. That literal is now the named
`CTC_PER_CHILD_SS24H2` and is **pinned against the year's package** by
`ctc_per_child_tests::the_named_ceiling_is_the_years_own_figure`. It cannot bite today
(`full_return_for` returns `Some` for TY2024 alone, and this predicate is only reached on a return the
absolute chain computed) — and the pin is what keeps that true: the moment TY2025's or TY2026's
package is bundled the test REDS, which is correct, because at $2,200 the ceiling here is too LOW and
the proof would conclude *provably zero* for a household that still has credit left — a sworn `0` on
line 19, taxpayer-adverse and invisible on the page. The fix then is to thread the package into
`ctc_odc_line19`, not to edit the number.

## Deviations, all deliberate

1. **`CreditColumn { ChildTaxCredit, CreditForOtherDependents, Neither }`, not `{ Ctc, Odc, None }`.**
   `None` as a variant shadows `Option::None` under a glob import, and the doctrine names a
   transcription for the words the form prints.

2. **25 cells mapped, not 41: rows (1)–(4) are DERIVED and deliberately NOT mapped.** They are the
   page-1 identity block's cells, and this build maps no TY2025 identity block — so a map entry for
   them would be a cell nothing writes, which is the *"blank because nothing populated it"* defect
   with a map entry in front of it. They stay unaccounted on the register with an exact count, and the
   generator prints their measured names **commented and UNQUOTED** (the census text-scans quoted
   spans, comments included, so a quoted comment would shrink the register by cells nothing writes).
   Fail-closed: with no mapped cell for a name, an SSN printed beside a blank name is not expressible.
   Rows (1)/(2) additionally carry a COLLECTION gap — the TY2025 form asks first and last name in two
   cells and `Dependent` holds one `name` string; splitting at a space would invent testimony. Filed
   as **FR-84**, owned by the TY2025 identity block.

3. **`hoh_qualifying_child_name` is live iff `HoH`**, not additionally gated on *"no dependent row is
   the qualifying person"* — btctax cannot evaluate that conjunct. A return may carry a dependent
   parent under Test 1 and still be qualified by a non-dependent child under Test 2; gating on
   `dependents.is_empty()` would hide the cell from exactly that household, and hiding a cell the IRS
   asks for is the silent-omission direction. The blank is lawful, so nothing refuses on it and the
   help says when to leave it blank.

4. **The five *Married persons who live apart* conditions were NOT transcribed** (the brief made it
   optional). `MarriedLivedApart` refuses, naming the rule and listing its five conditions in the
   refusal text so the filer can go and read it.

5. **`SkippableQuestion` gained a declared class.** Recorded above; the alternative (a `Choice` shape
   on `FormQuestion`) would have touched 61 literals instead of 20, and a hand-list of "the class-(A)
   ones" beside the registry is the shape this repo keeps getting wrong.

6. **`InterviewState` gained `not_computed`** rather than reusing `Forgo`, which is keyed by
   `AnswerKey` — a computed forgo has no answer key.

7. **`FullReturnParams` gained two fields** rather than the panel reading a constant. The
   `ctc_provably_zero` literal could not be threaded through `PrintedInputs` without a signature
   change to the printed chain, so it is NAMED and PINNED instead (§5 above).
