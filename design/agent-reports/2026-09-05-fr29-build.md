## 7. Where I departed from the spec, and why

1. **G8's `tax_year != year` rows cannot assert the invariant unconditionally, and the spec asks them
   to.** §4.2 (M-2) says in one breath that a hand-built `tax_year > year` inflates the computed age,
   suppresses the question and **bricks the filer** — *"the brick §3.1 exists to avoid"* — and in the
   next that G8 should assert demanded ⊆ live *"over a grid that includes `tax_year != year`"*. Those
   cannot both hold: the gate is handed the authoritative `year` and liveness has only `ri.tax_year`,
   so a `year + 5` row genuinely violates the invariant. **Measured, not argued**: the grid failed on
   `(dob=2004-06-15, Single, interest, tax_year=2029)`.
   **Resolution: assert the invariant, and key its ONE permitted escape to the MECHANISM rather than
   to a row list.** When a demanded question is not live, the test asserts that the two years
   *disagree about `provably_24_or_older`* and that `tax_year != year`. An income-dependent liveness
   term — the mutation G8 exists to kill — makes the crypto-only fixture dead with both years
   *agreeing*, so it still reds (verified: G8 is RED in the table above). The exposure is bounded to
   hand-built inputs, because `btctax-cli/src/return_inputs.rs` stamps `ri.tax_year = year` on load
   and errors on a stored disagreement.
2. **G14(a)'s polarity row lives in `btctax-input-form`.** Crate direction: it must go in through the
   `Field`, and `form_spec()` is in the crate that depends on core. Named in both directions.
3. **G14(a) — `c4 == Some(No)` PROCEEDS**, so "every other combination refuses" is scoped to
   `c3 == Some(true) && c4 != Some(No)`. Ladder step 5 is a proceed, and the test says so; the
   *certification* assertion (`form8615_certification().is_none()`) still covers every non-certified
   combination, including both proceed rows.
4. **P-1 (register this spec with `cite-check`) and P-2 (the `#[ignore]`d ~104-household corpus) were
   NOT done** — the brief scoped them out as separate tasks. OQ-2 (Charts A/B/C) stays deferred.
