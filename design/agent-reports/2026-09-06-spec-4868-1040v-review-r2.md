## (b) C-1 / I-6 — the recorded payment as the `--pay` default

**The field, verified:** `crates/btctax-core/src/tax/return_inputs.rs:708`

(`get`) / `:1048` (`set`) and the import fixture
`crates/btctax-cli/tests/fixtures/examples/fullreturn_inputs.toml:97`. R3's "this spec changes nothing
there" is **correct**.

**Is the default sound when the recorded amount exceeds line 6? YES, and nothing needs to refuse or
warn.** Two facts make it sound:

1. Line 5 is `line33 − Sch3 L10`, and line 33 *includes* Sch3 L10 (via L31 ← Sch3 L15 = L10 + L11,
   `printed.rs:1545`). So **lines 4, 5 and 6 are invariant to whether the payment is recorded** —
   the subtraction the form demands is exactly what makes a reprint stable.
2. Line 7 asks *"Amount you're paying"*, not *"amount you owe"*. A filer who records $5,000 against a
   $2,000 line 6 is over-paying, and $5,000 is the **true** testimony for line 7. Refusing or warning
   would be telling a filer their own recorded fact is wrong.

The spec also keeps `--pay` above line 6 explicitly allowed with its reason, which is consistent.
One seam is open — the default's source can carry cents while cents are refused (**N-M2, Minor**).

**FR-59's blast radius: plausible, and slightly short.** Spot-checked three of the ten:

## (g) I-4 / T5 — the row-band reader rule ✗

Three separate measurements, and T5 does not survive any of them.

**1. `f4868--2025` — the label reader MIS-JOINS 12 of 17 boxes today.** `xtask label-boxes
f4868--2025` (this is `label_join`'s own output):

```
…PartI_ReadOrder[0].f1_4[0]   5     ← line 1, "Your name(s)"
…PartI_ReadOrder[0].f1_5[0]   6     ← the address line
…PartI_ReadOrder[0].f1_6/7/8  8     ← city / state / ZIP
…PartI_ReadOrder[0].f1_9[0]   9     ← line 2, taxpayer SSN
…PartI_ReadOrder[0].f1_10[0]  9     ← line 3, spouse SSN
f1_11→4  f1_12→5  f1_13→6  f1_14→7  c1_1→8  c1_2→9    ← Part II, correct
```

**The 4868 is the corpus's first TWO-COLUMN form** — Part I (lines 1/2/3 + address) on the left, Part
II (lines 4–9) on the right, sharing row bands. `witness_text` returns exactly ONE column
(`label_reader.rs:275-295`, scored by distinct labels yielded), and here the Part II margin wins 7
labels to Part I's 3. The Part I boxes then fall through `label_join`'s in-row rule (their labels are
captions above, not within 12pt to the left) onto the column fallback — which is the *other column's*
label.

So T5's stated measurement, **"lines 1–3 do not join"**, is false: they join, to labels 5, 9 and 9.
And `every_mapped_line_lands_on_its_own_printed_label` — verified green today at
`2024: 261 join(s), 1 unreachable` / `2025: 215 join(s), 0 unreachable` — asserts `label_matches(key,
printed)` for **every** `lineN` binding (`label_reader.rs:1568-1626`), with the failure text *"a filled
value would print on the WRONG LINE of a signed return"*. A f4868 map binding `line1`/`line2`/`line3`
reds it three times. This is a **fourth row gate the spec does not name**, and r2 silently dropped
r1's parenthetical "one key per line above, **in the form's numbering**" from R1, leaving the choice
unstated at exactly the point where one option reds a gate and the other (`[header]`-style named keys,
as `forms/2024/f1040.map.toml:118-130` does for unnumbered cells) quietly abandons the form's own
numbering for three lines the form numbers — against this repo's transcription rule.

**2. `f1040v--2025` — the proposed rule yields 0 joins, not 4.** T5 says: *"when no label column
exists but ≥ 3 numeric labels sit in one row band, the x-aware in-row rule alone joins them — with the
kill on `f1040v--2025` (4 joins)"*. Replaying `label_join`'s exact in-row predicate (`lp == bp && ly >=
top - 2.0 && ly2 <= bottom && lx2 <= left + 1.0 && left - lx2 <= 12.0`, `label_reader.rs:1071-1080`)
against `design/forms/geometry/f1040v--2025.json` with `primary` empty gives **0 joins over all 15
boxes**. The measured geometry says why:

| | label | box | why in-row fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| box 1 | `1` at y 627.17–635.52, x2 59.29 | `f1_1` top-down 648.00–660.00, left 50.40 | the label is a **caption above** — `ly >= top − 2.0` is false by 21pt |
| box 2 | `2` at y 627.17–635.52, x2 203.29 | `f1_2` 648.00–660.00, left 194.40 | same |
| box 3 | `3` at y 627.17–635.52, x2 331.20 | `f1_3` 636.00–660.00, left 460.80 | above **and** 129.6pt to the left — 10× the calibrated 12pt window |
| box 4 | `4` at y 661.17–669.52, x2 58.29 | `f1_5` 670.00–684.00, left 51.40 | caption above again |

The 1040-V's four labels are **not** "inline in one row band beside their boxes"; they are
top-left cell captions. Consequence if T5 is built as written: `label_join` returns `Ok` with every
box `"?"`, the walk counts `unlabelled` and **not** joins, and `map_reach_problem(false, k, e, 0)`
fires the hard assert *"{e} binding(s) extracted, 0 joined — the join never examined them (the R2
shape)"* (`label_reader.rs:1195-1197`). One red is traded for another.

**3. The two escapes r1 named are both blocked, and one ratchet is missed.**
`GRID_MAPS` (`label_reader.rs:1138-1168`) requires **zero** numbered line keys — `(true, k, _, _) =>

## (h) I-5 — `return_due` and the two-month shift

- `YearRecord.return_due: time::Date` — `crates/btctax-forms/src/year_record.rs:73`, doc *"The
  unextended return due date for this tax year (a TOML date, e.g. `2026-04-15`)"*. ✅
- TY2025's record **is** `return_due = 2026-04-15` (`forms/2025/YEAR.toml:8`); TY2024's is
  `2025-04-15`; TY2017's is `2018-04-17`. ✅
- The form's sentence is exact and cited correctly: *"file your return or this form by June 15, 2026."* ✅

Sourcing the date from the record is right and fixes the hardcoded `-04-15`. **"Shifted two months" is
the wrong rule in general**, though it happens to be right for the two years that matter: the
out-of-country date is the fifteenth day of the sixth month (Reg §1.6081-5(a)(5)/(6)) with the §7503
weekend/holiday shift applied independently — **not** `return_due + 2 months`. TY2017 is the very
counterexample the spec cites to prove nothing is hardcoded: `2018-04-17 + 2 months = 2018-06-17`, but
the real out-of-country date for TY2017 was **2018-06-15**. TY2025 (2026-04-15 → 2026-06-15) and
TY2026 (2027-04-15 → 2027-06-15) are both correct. Recorded as **N-M3 (Minor)** — it changes only
whether a *warning* prints, on at most two days, in a year (TY2017) that refuses before reaching the
code path. **I-5 RESOLVED with a Minor.**
