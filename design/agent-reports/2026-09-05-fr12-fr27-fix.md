## 1. What the forms actually require, before any code

Read from the text layer (`pdftotext -layout`), never the rendered page.

**Form 8960 Part II** (`design/forms/extract/f8960--2024.txt:40-45`):

```
 9a    Investment interest expenses (see instructions)                    9a
   b   State, local, and foreign income tax (see instructions)            9b
   c   Miscellaneous investment expenses (see instructions)               9c
   d   Add lines 9a, 9b, and 9c                                          9d
10     Additional modifications (see instructions)                       10
11     Total deductions and modifications. Add lines 9d and 10           11
12     Net investment income. Subtract Part II, line 11, from Part I,
       line 8 … If zero or less, enter -0-                               12
```

**Form 1040** (`design/forms/extract/f1040--2025.txt`, lines 110, 112, 114, 115 — the block is
interleaved with the standard-deduction margin, so the four are quoted here in form order):

```
19   Child tax credit or credit for other dependents from Schedule 8812
20   Amount from Schedule 3, line 8
21   Add lines 19 and 20
22   Subtract line 21 from line 18. If zero or less, enter -0-
```

Note what is present and what is absent. **Part II of Form 8960 carries no *"enter -0-"* clause
anywhere** — the first one on the whole form is line 12's. **Lines 19 and 20 of the 1040 carry none
either** — lines 15 and 22 do. Under this repo's own census taxonomy that settles both productions:

* `Production::Combine` — *"Add lines X through Y … **with no clamp clause**. Blank iff every operand
  is blank."*
* `Production::Carry` — *"Enter the amount from line N" — **blank when the source line is blank**.*

**Both defects were the code contradicting its own census entry.** 8960 line 9d is registered
`Combine`; 1040 line 20 is registered `Carry`. The census records a line's *production* and never
checks a *value*, so nothing red. That is the "green and blind instrument" shape, and it is worth
saying plainly because the finding was already written down in the tree and had been for months.

### The three cases per line

| line | case | required | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **8960 L9d** | 9b unallocated | **BLANK** | 9a and 9c are unmodelled — no field exists in `Form8960Lines` at all — and 9b is `None`. A sum with no operands is not a figure. |
| | 9b allocated | **the figure** | One live operand ⇒ the form's arithmetic reaches the line and asks for it. |
| **1040 L20** | no Schedule 3 filed | **BLANK** | There is no Schedule 3 line 8 to name. |
| | Schedule 3 filed | **its line 8** | A true carry, against a page in the packet. |

## 2. FR-27's hard part, answered rather than dodged

The brief was right that this is the honest difficulty: a `0` on line 20 is *arithmetically* right
whenever the filer genuinely has no nonrefundable credits, and "the filer has none" and "btctax does
not model it" are the same blank page.

**The absence is never provable, and the code already knew it.** Schedule 3 line 8 is *"Add lines 1
through 4, 5a, 5b, and 7"*, and v1 models exactly **one** of those operands — line 1, the foreign tax
credit. Dependent-care (2441), education (8863), saver's (8880), residential clean energy and energy
efficient home improvement (5695), adoption (8839) and every §6a–6z other credit are §3.4
conservative omissions. Which is precisely why `Advisory::OtherCreditsOmitted` is pushed
**unconditionally**, with this comment beside it:

> *"v1 captures no input that could establish eligibility, so it cannot know whether this filer
> qualifies, only that it did not try."*

So there is no `Some(0)`-because-provably-empty branch to build, and the predicate reduces to the one
fact core does hold: **was a Schedule 3 actually filed?** That is `sch_3.map(|s| s.line8)` in
`printed.rs`. This is *not* the "quick unconditional blank" the brief warned against — `Some` is a
live branch with its own household in the KAT (below), and an unconditional `None` reds on it.

The FR-1 analogue is exact. There, Schedule 8812 line 12-No *instructs* `-0-` and line 14 routes it to
1040 line 19, so `Some(0)` is a real case. Here the real case is a filer whose Schedule 3 exists for
**Part II** alone (a Form 4868 extension payment): the schedule is in the packet, its line 8 prints
`0`, and line 20 carries a figure a reader can check against the attached page.

## 3. The change

**Both decisions are carried by the TYPE, and both are made in `btctax-core`.**

`crates/btctax-core/src/tax/other_taxes.rs` — `Form8960Lines::line9d: Option<Usd>`, and the site is
`let line9d = line9b;`. Not `unwrap_or(ZERO)`, which is the affirmative zero the item exists to stop.

`crates/btctax-core/src/tax/printed.rs` — `Form1040Lines::line20: Option<Usd>`, and the site is
`let line20 = sch_3.map(|s| s.line8);`. Line 21 becomes
`line19.unwrap_or(ZERO) + line20.unwrap_or(ZERO)`.

`crates/btctax-forms/src/form8960.rs` and `form1040_full.rs` — the fill sites are one-liners that pass
the `Option` straight to `push_money_opt`:

```rust
(lines.line9d, F8960_COL_AMOUNT),
(need(&map.line20, "line20", y)?, lines.line20),
```

**`btctax-forms` contains no tax reasoning of any kind.** No "does this household have a credit?"
predicate, no schedule-existence test — it transcribes an `Option` it is handed. Both lines stay *in*
their descent arrays with their ordinals intact rather than moving to a conditional push, for the
reason FR-1 recorded: `verify_flat` checks per-group ordinal-y descent, and hoisting an optional line
out of the sequence renumbers every line below it on one branch and not the other.

**No tax figure moves, on either fix.** 8960 line 11 and 1040 line 21 take a blank operand as nothing,
so lines 12 and 22 are byte-identical for every household and total tax is unchanged. Both fixes
*remove an assertion*; neither changes an amount.

### Blast radius the compiler found (free, and exactly the point)

`Form8960Lines` literals: `line_coverage.rs`. `Form1040Lines` literals: `line_coverage.rs`,
`return_1040.rs` (`the_lift_moves_no_printed_line`), `btctax-forms/tests/{extract_lines,
full_return_forms}.rs`. `Coverage::line` already took `impl Into<Option<Usd>>` (widened by FR-1), so
both census entries kept their `deny(unused_variables)` consumption guarantee with no `unwrap_or` that
would read like a claim about the figure.

## 4. Test-first, and mutation-verified separately

**Both KATs were written first and were red on exactly the fabricated `0`** before any fix:

```
form_8960_line_9d_is_blank_unless_the_filer_allocated_something_to_part_ii
  … "Add lines 9a, 9b, and 9c" carries no "-0-" clause … Paper: Some("0")

  … with no Schedule 3 there is no line 8 … Paper: Some("0")
```

Both are in `crates/btctax-forms/tests/attestation.rs`, both read the **emitted PDF**, and both carry
two households — the two sides of the one instruction, so that neither an unconditional `0` nor an
unconditional blank passes:

* **FR-12** — Single, $300k wages, $6k interest, itemizing with $20k state income tax (the P3-1
  fixture, reused so that "does this household file Form 8960 at all?" is already settled elsewhere).
  9b unallocated ⇒ `line9d` **absent** from the paper. 9b = $7,000 ⇒ `line9d` prints **`7000`**.
* **FR-27** — Single, $60k wages. No extension payment ⇒ no `f1040s3` in the packet and `line20`
  **absent**. `payments.extension_payment = $1,000` ⇒ `f1040s3` files, its `line8` reads `0`, and the
  1040's `line20` is asserted **equal to that cell**, so the carry and its source cannot drift apart.

### Two assertions were ENCODING the defects

Worth calling out, because both read like checks on the very line they were pinning wrong:

* `return_1040.rs::an_unanswered_line9b_neither_refuses_nor_prints_a_zero` asserted
  `f8960.line9d == Usd::ZERO` with the comment *"9d = 9a + 9b + 9c over three blanks"*.
* `full_return_forms.rs::f8960_line9b_is_blank_when_unclaimed_and_printed_when_claimed` asserted the
  cell reads `Some("0")` off the serialized PDF.

And on the 1040 side, `ALL_ZERO_1040_PAPER`'s `("line20", "0")` row: that table is an **equality**, so
a row in it is what makes a printed zero *mandatory*. The row was the bug, not the omission — the same
shape FR-1 found one line up. It is replaced by an explicit `!contains_key("line20")` carrying the
reason.

## 6. The one thing I did NOT do, and why — FR-39, filed

**Both fixes push the same question one line down, onto a `Combine` total whose operands are now all
blank-capable:**

* **1040 line 21**, *"Add lines 19 and 20"* — blank 19 (FR-1) and blank 20 (FR-27).
* **Form 8960 line 11**, *"Total deductions and modifications. Add lines 9d and 10"* — blank 9d
  (FR-12) and an unmodelled 10.

Both still print `0`, so the emitted paper now shows a figure on a total whose every operand is empty.
Under the census's own `Combine` rule — *"blank iff every operand is blank"* — both should go blank.

**I stopped, and this is the judgment call a reviewer should look at first.** FR-1 decided line 21
EXPLICITLY, in `5094bfc5`: *"a blank operand contributes nothing to the sum, which
is what a blank means in an addition on this form — so L21 stays a `Usd` and stays printed: line 22
subtracts it unconditionally."* That reasoning was **sound when line 20 was unconditionally present**;
it is now incomplete rather than wrong. An implementer sent to fix line 20 does not silently reverse a
reviewed decision on line 21 — and line 11 and line 21 are one shape, so they move together or not at
all.

Filed as **FR-39** with the full reasoning. It is not a loose end: both cells are **pinned as `0` by
tests that carry a comment pointing at FR-39** (`ALL_ZERO_1040_PAPER`'s `line21` row and
`full_return_forms.rs`'s `assert_eq!(tv(&pdf, L11), Some("0"))`), and the field docs on
`Form1040Lines::line21` and `Form8960Lines::line11` say the same. Whichever way it is ruled, the change
is one grep and it cannot be made to half of the pair.

Direction, for triage: same as its parents — a `0` on a credit/deduction total can only forgo and
OVERSTATE tax, and no figure moves either way, since lines 12 and 22 subtract a blank as nothing.

## 8. What a reviewer should look at first

1. **FR-39 — the stop.** Is leaving 8960 line 11 and 1040 line 21 printing `0` over all-blank operands

2. **Is `sch_3.map(|s| s.line8)` the right predicate for FR-27**, i.e. is "no Schedule 3 in the packet"

   regeneration diff is in §5.
