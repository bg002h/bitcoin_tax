## What changed vs TY2024 — exactly one thing

`dump-fields` on both years, diffed: **72 fields in each year, and the output differs on exactly two
lines.**

```
-p1  539.4, 133.0- 549.4, 143.0  btn  topmostSubform[0].Page1[0].c1_1[0]  on=["1"]
-p1  561.0, 133.0- 571.0, 143.0  btn  topmostSubform[0].Page1[0].c1_1[1]  on=["2"]
+p1  539.4, 133.0- 549.4, 143.0  btn  topmostSubform[0].Page1[0].TagcorrectingSubform[0].c1_1[0]  on=["1"]
+p1  561.0, 133.0- 571.0, 143.0  btn  topmostSubform[0].Page1[0].TagcorrectingSubform[0].c1_1[1]  on=["2"]
```

**The line-7a Yes/No pair moved one subform deeper**, into `TagcorrectingSubform[0]`. Every other name,
every `/Rect` to the tenth of a point, every on-state and the single `/MaxLen` are unchanged. Critically,
`c1_2` (the FBAR sub-question) and `c1_3` (line 8) did **not** move — so the three Part III pairs no
longer share a parent, and a name generated by analogy from a sibling pair is wrong. This is precisely
the failure the TY2024 map's own header warned about for the two payer tables, one year later: a TY2024
map copied forward **parses cleanly, looks right, and silently writes line 7a nowhere** — a foreign-account
answer that never reaches the page. `full_return_forms`-style verification would not necessarily catch it
either, since a blank checkbox pair is the common, correct case for most filers.

The printed form itself is unchanged. `diff` of the two committed text layers is: the year stamps
(2024→2025 on the face, inside 7a's "At any time during …" and 8's "During …"), a new "Created 4/23/25"
footer stamp, and `pdftotext` column reflow. No line added, removed, renumbered or reworded; same
cross-references to Form 1040 lines 2b and 3b, which the TY2025 Form 1040 line-11/12 renumbering does
not touch because Schedule B cites neither.

This matches `design/ty2025/recon-year-port-delta.md`'s classification of Schedule B as **TRIVIAL**
("only year stamps changed; the 4 field-geometry diffs are one checkbox pair nested one subform level
deeper — cosmetic"). One correction to that recon's wording: the nesting is **not cosmetic** at the map
layer. It is the whole port.

## Finding — a false claim in the TY2024 map, NOT carried forward

The TY2024 census reason for `f1_66` reads, verbatim:

> "Not a silent truncation: an over-long value FAILS CLOSED at verify.rs:516, so a list needing two
> lines refuses rather than clipping."

Both halves are false, and were false in 2024 as well:

- **`verify.rs:516` has decayed.** That line is now inside `find_yes_no_pair` (`crates/btctax-forms/src/verify.rs`),
  not any overflow guard.
- **The overflow guard cannot fire on this cell.** It is leg (5) of `verify_flat`
  (`verify.rs:422–437`), and it `continue`s unless the field declares `/MaxLen`. `dump-fields` reports
  **exactly one** `/MaxLen` on this entire form — the SSN cell `f1_02`, `maxlen=11`. Neither 7b cell
  declares one, so `FormsError::CellOverflow` is unreachable there, and no other leg of `verify_flat`
  checks text width. The write path (`schedule_b.rs:185–194`) pushes the whole collected string into
  `f1_65` as a `FlatPlacement::free`.

So a country list too long for one printed line is a **rendering risk, not a refusal**. I replaced the
reason with what is actually true and left a `★★ CORRECTED` note above the entry recording the old
claim, why it is wrong, and that the correction is a reason change and not a count change. I did **not**
reclassify the field as `rule = "gap"`: the datum *is* collected and *is* written, so this is an emitter
capacity limit rather than "a required declaration btctax never asks", and the gap ratchet
(`recorded_gaps_may_only_shrink`, pinned at 0, scanning year 2024 only) is a register the controller
owns. **Flagged for the controller, not silently decided.** The identical false reason is still live in
`crates/btctax-forms/forms/2024/f1040sb.map.toml`, which I did not touch — it is outside my scope.
