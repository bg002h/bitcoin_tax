### Controller's recorded decision (deviation D1) — the four T5 rows

Implemented exactly as briefed: `transcribed_rows` returns `Some(n)` **only** for `w2`; `None` for
every other kind, and `int_1099` / `div_1099` / `b_1099` / `g_1099` are live with `Some(true)`

**★ The tension this creates, stated plainly for the review.** `int_1099` / `div_1099` / `g_1099` /
`b_1099` are `Vec`s that `income import` fills TODAY and that the printed return uses today (1040
line 2b, Schedule B, Schedule D 1a/8a). Under the decision, the truthful answer for a filer who has
imported a 1099-INT (`Some(true)`) **refuses the return**, and the only fileable answer is
`Some(false)` — i.e. *"I received no Form 1099-INT"* beside $45,000 of transcribed 1099-INT
interest. Two committed fixtures now carry exactly that state and say so in a comment:
`crates/btctax-core/src/tax/scrub_axis.rs` (the scrub axis sentinel) and
`crates/btctax-cli/tests/fixtures/examples/nine_dependents_amt_inputs.toml`. It cannot be refused
today because `transcribed_rows` is `None` for those kinds, which is precisely what keeps the golden
corpus fileable — the decision is internally consistent, and it is also the reason a false census
answer is currently unrefusable.

*One-sentence recommendation, not acted on:* a narrower rule would refuse `Some(true)` on those four
only when **zero** rows are transcribed (no screen to enter them) and accept `Some(true)` when rows
ARE present (the filer entered them via `income import`), which refuses the same filer T5 exists for

**The 2024 f1040 direction key found (brief asked for this explicitly).** `Income` is *not* on a
line of its own in either revision: it is the left-margin caption printed as the **leading token of
the line-1a row** — `f1040--2024.txt:49` (` Income               1a      Total amount from Form(s)
W-2, box 1 …`, one leading space) and `f1040--2025.txt:57` (no leading space). The caption check is
whitespace-normalised and prefix-based, so both years key identically with no per-year special case.
No fallback to the form title exists — Form 1040 mixes directions (lines 1–9 vs 25–38), so a lost
caption **reds** (kill K13).

**No `[direction]` table on 2025 `f1040` or 2025 `schedule_d`:** both carry no `[census]` at all
(they are on `field_census.rs`'s `UNCENSUSED` register at 196 and 24), so they have zero `unmodeled`
entries. The join asserts the biconditional in both directions.

### Deviations on the table's shape

- **D9 — a third direction, `NoDollar`.** A third of the Form 1040's unmodelled cells carry no
  dollar (a fiscal-year date, a designee name, a phone number). Grading them `Overstates` would be
  false; grading them `Understates` would demand a refusal for a blank that cannot move the tax.
  R2.2's own parenthesis makes the same distinction for Schedule B Part III.
- **D9b — placement, and the anti-laundering rule.** An entry whose `line` carries a printed line
  label (one or two digits + optional letter, bounded) is placed by that label alone and **may not**
  carry `part` — otherwise an income line could be moved into a no-dollar bucket by one TOML key.
  An entry with no label (17 of them: `FY begin`, `designee Yes`, `1099-K`, …) **must** carry
  `part = "<caption>"` naming a block in its own table. Both directions are asserted.
- **D9c — no "dead block" rule, deliberately.** Schedule D's Part III heads lines 16–22, every one
  of which btctax fills, so the block grades nothing and is still the correct reading of the form.
  Deleting it to satisfy a liveness rule would delete the heading that must red when a Part III line
  later goes unmodelled. Drift is caught where drift shows: the caption assertion and the range
  assertion. Recorded in the code at the site.
- **D10 — the tables are larger than the spec's illustrative table.** §2.2's table lists one key for
  f1040 (`Income`); the f1040 census reaches the header, page 2 and the trailer, so ten blocks were
  needed, and Schedule 1's unnumbered 1099-K reconciliation line needed a block of its own
  (`For 2024, enter the amount reported to you on Form(s) 1099-K`, a caption the extract does print).

## 4. The join rules (R2.2)

**New `crates/xtask/src/census_join.rs`**, exposed as `cargo run -p xtask -- census-join` and run
under `make check` by its own `#[test]`s.

- `verdict(...)` is a **pure function** over `(blocks, census, extract lines, variant set, prompts)`,
  so every rule is reachable by a planted defect without mutating a committed map.
- Variant existence is read from the three enums' **source** (`advisories.rs`, `return_refuse.rs`,
  `questions.rs`) — a Rust enum cannot be iterated and two of the three have no `ALL`.
- `Understates` ⇒ an `Advisory` cover **reds**. `QuestionId` ⇒ **reach**: the covering question's
  prompt must contain the entry's keyword, which is `names` when present else the leading phrase of
  `reason` up to the first em dash. `RefuseReason` ⇒ existence. `Overstates` / `NoDollar` ⇒
  existence for all three.
- **The `names` guard.** A keyword must be ≥4 characters **and appear in the entry's own `reason`**,
  so it is a keyword OF THE LINE rather than a token chosen to satisfy the checker. Six terse
  *"— same."* reasons were extended (deviation D12) so their keyword is present rather than the rule
  weakened: f1040 `16 box 3 form`; f1040s1 `8z type` (the first cell); f1040s2 `1c`, `1e(ii..iv)`,
  `1f(ii..iv)`, `1y amount`, `17a amount`, `17z type`, `17z amount`.

**Why it lives in xtask (D8):** it reads `design/forms/extract/` (outside every published crate),
`btctax-core`'s enum sources and `btctax-forms`'s maps. An `include_str!` escaping a published crate
