## 4. What changed vs TY2024

Field **count** is identical (105 → 105) and 102 of 105 rows of `dump-fields` are byte-identical, so
a copy-the-file port looks correct. It is wrong in two places.

**(a) `c1_6` was RENAMED.** Line 1's "Statutory employee" checkbox:

```
2024  topmostSubform[0].Page1[0].c1_6[0]                        on=["1"]
2025  topmostSubform[0].Page1[0].Line1_ReadOrder[0].c1_6[0]     on=["1"]
```

Same rect (434.0, 518.0 – 442.0, 526.0), one extra subform level. The TY2024 spelling does not exist
on the TY2025 form; carrying it over makes the census entry a **phantom** and leaves the real widget
unaccounted for — both directions of the `field_census` gate red at once.

**(b) Lines 27a and 27b traded content. The field names did not move with the letters.**

| | TY2024 letter | TY2025 letter | field, both years |
|---|---|---|---|
| Other expenses (from line 48) | 27a | **27b** | `Lines18-27[0].f1_39[0]` |
| Energy efficient commercial bldgs deduction (Form 7205) | 27b | **27a** | `Lines18-27[0].f1_40[0]` |

Corroborated three independent ways in the TY2025 text layer:

1. the gutter marker beside each box — `f1_40` at y=252 prints `27a`, `f1_39` at y=240 prints `27b`;
2. line 48's own destination — TY2025: *"Total other expenses. Enter here and on line 27b"*
   (TY2024 said `27a`);
3. Part V's heading — TY2025: *"List below business expenses not included on lines 8-27a, or line
   30"* (TY2024: *"lines 8–26, line 27b, or line 30"*).

★ The boxes also **moved a row**: TY2024 had `f1_39` at y=264 and `f1_40` at y=240 with the y=252
right-column slot empty; TY2025 has `f1_40` at y=252 and `f1_39` at y=240 with y=264 empty. So a port
keyed to the printed letter is wrong, **and** a port keyed to the box position is wrong. Only a port
keyed to the field name survives — which is what this map does, with the TY2025 letters in the
census. Neither field is filled by btctax, so no dollar figure was at risk this year; the trap is
live for anyone who later maps them.

**(c) Year text** on lines G, H, I and line 44 ("during 2025", "payments in 2025").

Nothing else moved: line 28 still reads "Add lines 8 through 27b"; the money column is still
x ≈ [475.2, 576] on page 1 and [468, 576] on page 2; `/MaxLen` is unchanged on all six comb/limited
cells (SSN 11, NAICS 6, EIN 9, 43 MM/DD 2/2, 43 YYYY 4); page-1 Yes/No on-states are still
`"Yes"`/`"No"` while page-2's are `"1"`/`"2"` — re-dumped per pair, not inferred by analogy.

## 5. The line-J gate — checked, and it survived

The gate is **not in the map**; it is in `crates/btctax-core/src/tax/printed.rs`, in
`schedule_c_lines`:

```rust
line_j_1099_filed: h
    .payments_requiring_1099
    .and_then(|i| i.then_some(h.will_file_required_1099))
    .flatten(),
```

with the reason written above it ("an answer to J without a Yes on I is not a mark the form has a
place for … belt-and-braces here so a TOML import cannot produce a J-without-I page"), the matching
doc comment on `ScheduleCLines::line_j_1099_filed`, and the back-reference from the Schedule B FBAR
test at `printed.rs:3181`. **Present at HEAD, unmodified.** Read-only greps; no `git` was run.

What the map owes the gate is that **both** pairs stay declared. `schedule_c.rs` writes a pair only
under `if let (Some(pair), Some(answer))`, so deleting `[line_j]` from the map would silently discard
an answer the filer actually gave, with no error anywhere. `[line_i]` and `[line_j]` are both present
with the dumped `"Yes"`/`"No"` on-states, and that requirement is written into the map's comments so
the next porter meets it.

## 6. What I could not resolve — for the controller

1. **No committed test yet checks this file.** `field_census.rs::census_accounts_for_every_field`
   hardcodes `let year = 2024;`, and `map.rs` has no `SCHEDULE_C_MAP_2025` / `SCHEDULE_C_PDF_2025`
   constant and no `ScheduleCMap::ty2025()`. All three are Rust source, which this brief forbids me
   to touch. Until they exist, the §1 partition and the §2 line check hold only because I ran them by
   hand here — they are not yet a command anyone can re-run. **This is the one thing that must follow
   before the map can be trusted by anything but this report.**
2. **The map was never deserialized into `ScheduleCMap`.** Building is forbidden in this shared tree
   (nine agents, one cargo lock). It parses as TOML, its key set is exactly the struct's required
   fields plus `[census]`, and every value shape mirrors the TY2024 file that does deserialize — but
   `toml::from_str::<ScheduleCMap>` has not been executed.
3. **`label-census` / `label-boxes` are WRONG on this form, and quietly.** Schedule C's Part II is a
   **two-column** layout, and the reader picks a single label column: it reported 42 labels (missing
   every one of A–J, 18–27b, 20a/20b, 24a/24b, 44b/44c) and then assigned right-column boxes to
   left-column labels — `f1_28`→`8`, `f1_29`→`9`, `f1_40`→`16b`, `f1_39`→`17`. It printed no
   complaint. This is the documented weakness in `LABEL_READER.md` ("a form with two money columns
   puts some gutter labels ~95pt further left"), now measured on a second form. **I did not use its
   output for any assignment**; every line in the map comes from the per-box gutter read in §2. Worth
   an entry wherever that instrument's blind spots are tracked — a future porter who trusts
   `label-boxes` on Schedule C gets a confident, wrong map.
4. **`LIMITATIONS.md` is stale for lines I and J.** It still says *"Schedule C lines G, H, I, J — left
   blank (deferred to your pen)"*; I and J have been asked and printed since 2026-07-31. Accurate for
   G and H. Not mine to edit; noted in the map's comments too.
