# TY2025 AcroForm map batch — completeness audit

**Verdict: 0 Critical / 2 Important / 5 Minor / 2 Nit.**

No map in this batch would emit a wrong number on a signed return. Every field name in
every map exists in that year's PDF, every mapped line lands on the line the form
prints beside it, and every unmapped field carries a reason. The two Important findings
are a schema mismatch that makes one map unloadable (`f6251`) and one count claim in a
committed header that does not reconcile (`f8995`).

---

## What was machine-checked (and how)

All ten forms, `./target/debug/xtask dump-fields design/forms/2025/<stem>--2025.pdf`
against `crates/btctax-forms/forms/2025/<form>.map.toml`. No cargo, no make, no git.

| check | result |
|---|---|
| bundled `.pdf` sha256 == archived `design/forms/2025/*.pdf` | 10/10 identical |
| dump field count == agent-reported count | 10/10 (54, 63, 37, 33, 72, 105, 62, 26, 38, 33) |
| **phantom names** — a map FQN absent from the PDF | **0 of 481 references** |
| **unaccounted** — a PDF field neither mapped nor censused | **0** |
| a field both mapped and censused, or mapped twice | 0 |
| census entry missing `line` / `rule` / `reason` | 0 |
| `year = 2025` and `form` correct | 10/10 |
| **line↔box join** — mapped `lineN` vs the label the form prints beside the box | **0 mismatches** |
| mapped checkbox `on` state vs the widget's PDF on-state | 15/15 correct |
| map top-level keys satisfy the Rust `*Map` struct | **9/10 — `f6251` fails** |

The line↔box join was verified twice, independently of the agents' method: once with
`pdftotext -bbox` word boxes joined to the dumped widget rects (both the
leftmost-label-in-row and nearest-label-left-of-box witnesses), and again on the five
forms that have a geometry fixture with the repo's own `xtask label-boxes`. Both agree
on every mapped line of all ten forms.

The **year-port trap this repo keeps producing did not recur.** Spot-checks against the
TY2024 bundled PDFs confirm every renumbering the agents reported, in both directions:
Schedule 3 39→37 fields; Schedule A 37→33 with all 37 FQNs re-rooted `topmostSubform[0]`
→ `form1[0]`; Schedule 2 `f1_14` moved from a money box at [504,324] to the line-4
write-in at [252,312] under `Line4_ReadOrder[0]`; Schedule B exactly two dump rows differ
(the `c1_1` pair gaining `TagcorrectingSubform[0]`, with `c1_2`/`c1_3` unmoved); Schedule C
exactly three rows differ (`c1_6` gaining `Line1_ReadOrder[0]`, and `f1_39`/`f1_40`
trading rows at y=252/240); Form 6251 page 1 32→33 widgets with page 2 byte-identical;
Forms 8959 and 8960 zero diff. In every case the committed 2025 map uses the 2025 name.

## IMPORTANT 1 — `f6251` 2025 map cannot deserialize: `Form6251Map` still requires `line1`

**Form:** f6251
**File:** `crates/btctax-forms/forms/2025/f6251.map.toml` (keys `line1a`, `line1b`) vs
`crates/btctax-forms/src/map.rs:116` (`pub struct Form6251Map { … pub line1: MoneyCell, … }`)

**What is wrong.** TY2025 split Form 6251 line 1 into 1a/1b, and the map correctly
declares `line1a` and `line1b`. `Form6251Map` has a mandatory, non-`Option` `line1` and
no `line1a`/`line1b`. There is no `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields)]` and no `#[serde(alias)]`,
so `toml::from_str::<Form6251Map>` on this file fails with *missing field `line1`* while
silently discarding both new keys. This is the only one of the nine struct-backed maps
that does not fit; the schema check passes for all eighteen other year/form pairs.

Nothing emits a wrong number today only because nothing loads the file: there is no
`F6251_MAP_2025` const, and `crates/btctax-forms/src/packet.rs:179` hardcodes
`&crate::map::Form6251Map::ty2024()` with no year dispatch. **That hardcoding is the
Critical-in-waiting**: if the TY2025 6251 PDF is wired up without changing the struct,
the only map that parses is the TY2024 one, whose page-1 names are all off by one on the
2025 page — 2a lands in 1b's box, everything below shifts down, and line 11 (the AMT
itself, feeding Schedule 2 line 2) lands in line 10's box.

**Minimal fix.** Replace `pub line1: MoneyCell` with `pub line1a: MoneyCell` and
`pub line1b: MoneyCell`, add both to the exhaustive `money_cells()` destructure at
`map.rs:183` (which has no `..`, so the compiler will name every site), and give the
TY2024 map the same two keys pointing at its single line-1 box, or keep `line1` as
`Option` for 2024 alongside required-in-2025 handling. Do not wire a `F6251_MAP_2025`
until `packet.rs:179` selects by year.

## IMPORTANT 2 — `f8995` header claims 21 renamed fields; the measured number is 18

**Form:** f8995
**File:** `crates/btctax-forms/forms/2025/f8995.map.toml:6` and `:128`
("**the IRS renamed 21 of the 33 fields for TY2025**")

**What is wrong.** Diffing the two years' `dump-fields` name columns positionally gives
**18** changed FQNs of 33, not 21:

```
f1_1→f1_01, f1_2→f1_02                              (2, identity)
Ln1A_Row1→Row1i … Ln1E_Row5→Row1v, with f1_3..f1_9  (15, the five table rows × 3 leaves)
ReadOrderSubForm[0]→Line2_ReadOrder[0]              (1)
```

The header's own enumeration is substantively complete — it is only the headline count
that does not reconcile, and it is the number a future reader would check first. The map
body is unaffected: all 33 names are the TY2025 spellings and all 21 mapped cells land on
the right printed line.

**Minimal fix.** Change "21 of the 33" to "18 of the 33" at `f8995.map.toml:6` and `:128`.

## MINOR 1 — `f6251` agent report's "42 mapped / 20 unmapped" does not match the file

The committed map is right and self-consistent: `f6251.map.toml:184` says
"2 identity + 42 mapped + 18 here", which measures out exactly (44 mapped FQNs including
identity, 18 census entries, 62 total). The batch report's "42 mapped / 20 unmapped"
double-counts the two identity cells as unmapped. **Fix:** report wording only; no change
to the artifact.

## MINOR 2 — `f1040sc` census heading contradicts its own body and the file

`crates/btctax-forms/forms/2025/f1040sc.map.toml:136` opens
"★★ **TWO GAPS on this form (4 fields)**" and then states three lines later that the file
"records ZERO `gap` entries". `grep -c 'rule = "gap"'` on the file returns **0**, and the
parsed census contains only `unmodeled`. The heading is leftover TY2024 framing.
**Fix:** retitle to "NO GAPS on this form — how the TY2024 four were closed".

## MINOR 3 — the whole batch is inert, so no compiled consumer has ever checked it

None of the ten new files is `include_str!`'d: `crates/btctax-forms/src/map.rs` embeds
only `f8949`, `schedule_d`, `schedule_se`, `f8283` and `f1040` for 2025 (versus 17 files
for 2024). There is also **no `Schedule1AMap` struct anywhere in `btctax-forms`**, so the
54-field `f1040s1a` map has no deserialization target at all. Consequence: the f6251
schema break above would not have been caught by any existing test, and
`map_2025_matches_bundled_pdf_fieldset` (`tests/kats.rs:196`, `tests/sp2.rs:1262`) does not
reach these files. **Fix:** when each form is wired, add its const and extend that test's
fixture list; until then treat "N mapped" as an unexecuted claim.

## MINOR 4 — five of the ten forms have no geometry fixture, so the repo's own instrument cannot witness them

`design/forms/geometry/` holds only `f1040s1a--2025`, `f1040sa--2025`, `f1040sb--2025`,
`f1040sc--2025`, `f6251--2025`. `xtask label-census f1040s3--2025` (and `f8995`, `f8959`,
`f8960`, `f1040s2`) exits with *geometry fixture missing*. Those five maps' line↔box joins
are backed by the agents' own `pdftotext` work and by this audit's independent re-derivation,
but by no committed instrument. **Fix:** generate the five fixtures with
`xtask extract-geometry <stem>` so `label-census` covers all ten.

## MINOR 5 — `xtask label-boxes` is blind to Schedule C's right-hand Part II column

Run against the `f1040sc` census, `label-boxes` disagrees on 40 of 88 entries — it labels
`f1_28..f1_40` as lines 8..17 (the *left* column's gutter at the same y) when they are
lines 18..27b, and it collapses every Part V write-in row onto "47b". **The map is right
and the tool is wrong**: the widgets sit under `Lines18-27[0]`, and the counts are exact
(11 boxes for the 11 left-column labels 8..17, 13 for the 13 right-column labels 18..27b).
Recorded because it means the Schedule C census has no instrument witness, and because a
future reader running `label-boxes` will see a 40-row disagreement that is not a defect.
**Fix:** teach `label-boxes` to bucket by x-cluster before joining by y, or record the
known blindness in its output.

## NITS

1. `crates/btctax-forms/forms/2025/f1040s2.map.toml:100` cites `packet.rs:656`. Two files
   are named `packet.rs`; `crates/btctax-forms/src/packet.rs` is 240 lines long. The claim
   is **backed** — `crates/btctax-core/src/tax/packet.rs:656` is verbatim
   `let f6251 = ar.amt.must_attach().then(|| ar.amt.clone());` — but the bare path sends a
   reader to the wrong crate. Qualify it.
2. Two stale doc comments now contradicted by the batch: `map.rs:108` says Form 6251
   lines 2c–2t are "CENSUSED as `gap` (not `unmodeled`)" when both years' maps rule all 18
   `unmodeled`; `tests/field_census_slice.rs:295` says Schedule 1-A has "no `.map.toml`"
   when it now has one. Both pre-date this batch except for the second's newly-false half.

## Explicitly empty

**There are no Critical findings.** Every mapped line on all ten forms was checked against
the printed form by two independent geometric witnesses and found correct; no map would
put a figure on a line the IRS did not intend, and the batch cannot emit at all until it
is wired.
