Direction of the residual risk: a second trade or business would **add** QBI at line 2 and **increase**
the deduction, so leaving rows ii–v blank can only overstate tax, never understate it. That is the
`unmodeled` (benefit forgone) shape, not the `gap` (missing reduction) shape.

★ The `line =` labels here are **corrected** relative to TY2024. The TY2024 map wrote the three
columns as `1(i)B`/`1(ii)B`/`1(iii)B`, conflating the *column letters* `(a)/(b)/(c)` with the *row*
roman numerals; TY2025 uses `1(a)ii` etc., which is what the form prints. Nothing mechanical depended
on the old strings (the census scanner keys on the FQN), so this is a legibility fix, not a behaviour
change.

## 6. Gaps — none, and that is a checked fact

`tests/field_census.rs::recorded_gaps_may_only_shrink` pins `GAPS = 0` and permits only downward
movement. The TY2025 map introduces none. Specifically, **line 3** — the prior-year qualified business
net (loss) carryforward, *subtracted* at line 4 and therefore the one field on this form whose omission
would **understate** tax — is an input and is **mapped**: `crates/btctax-core/src/tax/qbi.rs` takes
`qbi_carryforward_in` and `Form8995Lines::line3` prints it (`qbi.rs:211` — *"Line 3 … **IS an input and
IS printed**"*). The 2026-07-30 closure carries forward intact.
