## Findings

**1. [Important] `handle_pseudo_approve_modal_key` is a FIFTH site that builds a decision payload from `app.snapshot` at write time; §0.1's "Four sites", §2.3 step 4's probe set and §3.3's gating KATs all exclude it.**
`main.rs:8209-8227`: `match (app.snapshot.as_ref(), app.session.as_ref())` → `btctax_core::pseudo_plan(&snap.events, prices, &cfg).into_iter().map(|pd| pd.decision).collect()` → a `Vec<EventPayload>` handed verbatim to `crate::edit::persist::persist_bulk_decisions(session, payloads, now, …)` at `:8242`. Its own doc comment states the design's exact anti-pattern: *"The plan is RE-derived here from the held snapshot"* (`:8201-8202`). Only the *filter* is session-derived in the bulk-income case §0.1 corrected — here the whole payload set is snapshot-derived, and the appended `ClassifyInbound`/income decisions carry FMV and kind, i.e. Schedule 1 / Form 8949 numbers. So §0.1's sentence "Four sites build a payload or a preview from `app.snapshot`; none reads `session`" is false as an enumeration of the defect. **Failure scenario:** the filer classifies an inbound (tail `:1661`), the re-projection fails, the latch arms; if the surface gating is ever relaxed (or is wrong today at some entry), `P`-Enter re-derives `pseudo_plan` off the pre-write `events`, re-proposes a synthetic default for the transfer the filer just classified, and appends it — a decision the current ledger would refuse. Today it is unreachable, but only through the two surface mechanisms (`pseudo_approve_modal` is in `close_all` at `:751`; `open_pseudo_approve_flow` gets a check in this very cycle) — which is precisely the "armed ⇒ unreachable" two-mechanism reasoning D-5 rejects for the other four. **Fix:** add `main.rs:8209-8227` to §0.1's table and to §2.3 step 4's probe set, and extend §3.3's gating KAT to "no pseudo-approve payload set can be built while armed", mutation-proven. Also state plainly what the four (five) probes actually are: since every payload site sits behind a surface `close_all` clears and an opener that refuses, **the probes are unreachable in production and the real guarantee is §2.3 steps 1-3 plus §3.5** — the KATs pin defence-in-depth, not the primary line.

**2. [Important] D-4's clear site is dead code, and the export — the only place a successful re-projection can happen while armed — is not specified to clear anything, so §2.6's data flow and §3.7's KAT describe behaviour the architecture cannot produce.**
§2.2 puts the clear in `apply_reprojection`'s `Some(Ok(..))` arm. `apply_reprojection` is reached only from `after_write`, i.e. from the 27 tails — and every one of those 27 sits behind a surface `close_all_mutation_surfaces` clears and an opener that takes the combined latch, so **no tail can run while armed** and the Ok-arm clear can never fire in production. The only re-projection reachable while armed is `execute_defensive_export`'s, at `main.rs:4626-4631`, which does its own `app.session.as_ref().map(btctax_tui::unlock::build_snapshot)` and `app.snapshot = Some(snap)` inline — §3.6 deliberately keeps it out of `after_write`. It therefore cannot be routed through `apply_reprojection` either: that helper's `Err` arm *arms* the latch and calls `close_all(true)`, whereas D-7 requires "Err ⇒ refuse, latch unchanged" with the bespoke refusal at `:4634-4638`, and its `Ok` arm sets `app.status`, which the export must not do (the status comes from `persist_defensive_export` at `:4685`). **Failure scenario:** built literally as specified, a filer arms from a Browse tail, presses `w` (allowed, D-7), presses `x`, the packet is written off a demonstrably fresh image (`apply_export` at `chokepoint/mod.rs:761-778` takes `&Session` and appends nothing, so nothing re-stales it) — and the marker still says the last write's effect is unverified, every opener still refuses, and fact 3 still tells them to quit and reopen into a `build_snapshot` that may bounce them (`unlock.rs:129-136`). This is arch-r3 m-10 / tax-r2 I-5 unchanged. **Fix:** name the export's `Some(Ok(..))` arm (`main.rs:4629-4632`) as an explicit, second clear site — `self.stale_after_write = None;` beside the assignment — restate D-4 as "one arm site, two clear sites (one of them the only reachable one)", and either delete the unreachable Ok-arm clear or record that it is fail-safe-only.

**3. [Important] §2.5's three-fact arm copy cannot render on either screen: Browse truncates it to ~80 characters and DefensiveFiling clips it at 3 rows now shared with the marker — so the remedy (fact 3) is invisible at all 27 tails.**
Browse's status surface is the single `Constraint::Length(1)` footer (`draw_edit.rs:188`, `:279-294`), rendered as `Paragraph::new(footer_text).alignment(Alignment::Center)` with **no** `.wrap(...)` — a line longer than the area is truncated, not wrapped. §2.4(b) gives Browse only a one-row *marker*; it never says where the facts go, so they land in that footer. On DefensiveFiling the notice is `DEFENSIVE_NOTICE_LINES = 3` (`draw_edit.rs:169`), whose own doc comment says 3 was chosen so "the longest shipped status (~230 chars) still renders in full on an 80-col terminal" — and §2.4(a) now additionally requires the marker to share that rect ("when a status is present, both appear"). The new copy is prefix + fact 1 (§2.5's mandated wording is ~128 chars on its own) + `({e})` + fact 2 + fact 3 ≈ 300-350 chars, against 3 × ~76 = ~228 usable. **Failure scenario:** the filer voids a decision on Browse (tail `:4809`), re-projection fails, and the footer shows `declared… the write reached disk, but whether it had the inte` — facts 2 ("your in-progress entry was closed") and 3 ("quit and reopen") never appear; on the declare/promote tails (`:4217`, `:4498`) fact 3 is clipped off the bottom of the notice. This is the fourth round on the D-2 delivery surface. **Fix:** raise `DEFENSIVE_NOTICE_LINES` to fit marker + longest arm status at 80 columns (measure the actual composed string, don't estimate), and on Browse either give the armed state a multi-row status band (a `Constraint::Length(n)` block, wrapped) or split the copy explicitly — short marker in the new row, full facts in a wrapped band — and KAT the *full* arm status rendering unclipped at 80 columns on both screens, not only the marker.

**4. [Important] §3.5(b)'s guard cannot express the property D-7 made load-bearing — that each opener holds the *right* latch — so a silent downgrade of any of the 25 combined openers to the original passes the guard and re-opens a filed-number path.**
The guard is stated as "every `fn open_*` … **contains a latch check**". Before D-7 there was one latch function, so presence was the property. Now there are two, and §2.3 step 3's split is the whole safety argument: `open_declare_flow` (`main.rs:3997`) and `open_promote_flow` (`:4253`) taking the **combined** latch is the only thing that keeps the dashboard — deliberately left alive by `close_all` and deliberately enterable while armed — from being a chain-A shaped path to a `DeclarePlan`/`PromotePlan`. A presence-only guard is green if someone "aligns" either opener with its sibling `open_defensive_filing`, which legitimately uses `residue_latch_status()` two files over. **Failure scenario:** a later cycle unifies the dashboard's three entry points on the original latch "for consistency"; the guard, the 25-key opener KAT (which only exercises `rollback_failed`, under which both latches refuse) and every §3.3 payload KAT (which arms the flow directly, bypassing the opener) all stay green — and a `DeclareTranche` planned off a stale `events` (`main.rs:4178-4188`, appended verbatim by `apply_declare`, `chokepoint/mod.rs:601-609`) lands as the synthetic acquisition behind a Form 8949 basis. **Fix:** specify the guard as an exact partition, not a presence test: the set calling `residue_latch_status()` must be exactly `{execute_defensive_export main.rs:4616, EditorApp::open_defensive_filing editor.rs:474}` and every other `fn open_*` must call `stale_or_residue_latch_status()`; assert both directions, and mutation-prove by flipping `open_declare_flow`.

**5. [Important] §3.5(b)'s domain (`fn open_*`) is not the set of surface-opening sites: twelve production sites assign a top-level `EditorApp` surface field from inside a `handle_*_key` fn and are structurally invisible to the scan.**
Enumerated over `NR ≤ 10706`: `mutation_modal` ← `handle_form_key:610`; `classify_inbound_modal` ← `:1862`, `:1994`, `:2111`; `reclassify_outflow_modal` ← `:2433`; `method_election_modal` ← `:2809`; `reclassify_income_modal` ← `:2976`; `set_fmv_modal` ← `:3116`; `void_modal` ← `:3282`; `select_lots_modal` ← `:5072`; `set_donation_details_modal` ← `:5426`; `link_transfer_modal` ← `:6093`; `classify_raw_modal` ← `:6438`, `:6564`; `resolve_conflict_modal` ← `:9976`. None matches `fn open_*`. Each is today reachable only from a parent flow that `close_all` clears, so there is no live exposure — but that is the invariant, and the design never states it; what it states is a guard over a name pattern. This is the same failure mode that produced chain A (the `_flow`/`_form` pattern missing `open_bulk_income_modal`), one level deeper. **Failure scenario:** a future surface is added whose parent is *not* in `close_all` (exactly how `bulk_income_flow` ended up outside it), the assignment lives in a `handle_*_key`, guard (b) is silent, and guard (a) — dispatch-set ⊆ clear-set — only catches it if the new surface also gets a `handle_key` dispatch line. **Fix:** define guard (b)'s domain as **every assignment of an `EditorApp` surface field to `Some(...)` in `main.rs` + `editor.rs`**, with the exempt list being "assigned only from inside a surface `close_all_mutation_surfaces` clears", each entry naming its parent — which is a grep-checkable claim, unlike a name pattern.

**Minor 1.** §3.5(a)'s stated justification for excluding `defensive_dashboard` from the clear set — "nulling it would kill D-7's route" — is false. With `open_defensive_filing` on the original latch (D-7), a filer whose dashboard was nulled presses `Esc` → Browse → `w` → the dashboard is rebuilt (`editor.rs:503-514`) → `x`. Nulling it would only cost the two on-screen arming tails an extra two keypresses, and `draw_defensive_filing` already has the fallback (`draw_edit.rs:144-148`). The exclusion is still *correct*, but for the real reason: every dashboard intent is individually gated (`Declare`/`Promote` → combined latch) or exempt by design (`Export`) or inert (`RouteResolveFirst`, `None`) — see `defensive_dashboard.rs:84-134`. Record the true rationale, or a future reader will "fix" guard (a) by adding the dashboard and believe they have killed D-7.

**Minor 2.** `open_defensive_filing`'s DFW-D6 gate reads `snap.state.pseudo_active()` off the **stale** image (`editor.rs:478-490`). From the pseudo-approve tail (`:8253`) — the one tail whose write changes exactly that predicate — the stale snapshot still reports pseudo-active, so `w` refuses with "Defensive Filing is unavailable: pseudo-reconcile synthetic defaults are contributing…", a claim about a ledger state the filer just cleared, and D-7's route is unreachable from that arming path. Either note the carve-out or have the gate re-read after a successful export-style rebuild.

**Minor 3.** §2.3 step 1's "`flush_tax_inputs_draft` itself refuses under **either** save-forbidding latch" is ambiguous against the `may_save` parameter. If "either" includes `stale_after_write`, then `apply_reprojection`'s `may_save = true` is a contradiction (the flush it authorises always refuses); if it means `residue_latch_status().is_some()`, the parameter is redundant with the flush's own check on the only caller that passes `false`. Say which. Separately, `may_save = true` is **vacuous in production**: `handle_key` dispatches `tax_inputs_form` at `:377`, before screen dispatch, so no other flow/modal can be opened while the form is live and no non-tax-inputs tail can run with a dirty form; at the two tax-inputs tails the form is already `None` (`:1340`) or `dirty = false` (`:1536`), and `flush_tax_inputs_draft` is dirty-gated (`:965-967`). **Minor 4.** §3.5(c)'s write-side gate names only `flush_tax_inputs_draft`, but three other production sites reach `Session::save()` outside it: `commit_tax_inputs`' `NoTables` arm calls `form_save_draft` directly (`main.rs:1381`), `confirm_park_to_profile` calls `form_park_to_profile` (`:1518`), and the discard path calls `form_discard_parked_draft` (`:1602`). **Nit 1.** The extended `kat_rollback_failed_latch_refuses_all_openers` (`:21381`, 9 → 25 keys) cannot be reused verbatim for the stale latch: under `stale_after_write`, `w` must **not** refuse (D-7). §3.5 should say the extension is 25 keys for `rollback_failed` and 24 (minus `w`) for the stale latch, with §3.7's positive `w`/`x` KAT covering the difference.

**Nit 2.** §2.4(a) specifies what the notice rect *renders* while armed but not that the **reservation** predicate at `draw_edit.rs:109` must change from `status.is_some()` to `status.is_some() || armed`. Without that there is no rect to render into.

**Nit 3.** At the park tail the `after_write` call must be inserted **after** the `:1532-1537` form block, not at the current `build_snapshot` position (`:1528-1531`) which precedes it — otherwise `close_all(may_save = true)` runs before `form.dirty = false` (`:1536`) and the flush stops being a no-op.

## Verified sound

- **The 27-tail set is exact.** My own scan of `build_snapshot(` over `NR ≤ 10706` reproduces §0.2's 27 line numbers with zero difference, and `:4626`'s paren-less `.map(btctax_tui::unlock::build_snapshot)` is correctly excluded.
- **The 19/7/1 taxonomy is correct.** Re-measured per-tail over each `Ok((snap, _))` arm: 19 snapshot-derived, 7 pure-literal, 1 hoisted. The two no-snapshot `derive_*` sites (`:5131`, `:9204`) are real and the "calls a `derive_*` ⇒ snapshot-derived" rule is genuinely unsafe.
- **The closure form is buildable at all 27.** Scripted over every `Ok((snap,…))` arm: the only `app.*` touched is `app.status`; the two `refresh_defensive_dashboard(app)` calls (`:4228`, `:4511`) are the generic post-install step the helper absorbs, and their `is_some()` guard is behaviour-preserving because `defensive_dashboard` is `Some` **iff** `screen == DefensiveFiling` (assigned `editor.rs:511` / `main.rs:4576`, nulled `main.rs:498` on every exit).
- **The `stale_reason()` probe compiles at all four sites.** The exact shape — an owned-return `&self` probe followed by `&mut` field writes — is already live at `main.rs:4616-4630` (`if let Some(s) = app.residue_latch_status() { app.status = Some(s); return; }` then `app.snapshot = Some(snap)`). At each of the four sites the probe is strictly additive: `app.snapshot.as_ref()` + `app.{declare,promote}_flow.as_mut()` are **disjoint field** borrows through `app: &mut EditorApp` and already compile today (that is why these sites never needed the accessor); the probe's borrow ends at the call, and `key`/`step` are `Copy` so neither match scrutinee holds an `app` borrow. `return` from `:4116` and `:4319` exits fns whose outer match is the last statement (`:4144-4145`, `:4330-4331`).
- **`may_save = true`'s "memory == disk" premise is true at all 27.** Every `Err` re-projection arm is nested in an `Ok(...)` persist result; every wrapper reaches disk via `save_or_rollback` → `session.save()` (`edit/persist.rs:227-232`) or its own `sess.save()`, including the one non-`save_or_rollback` path, `persist_safe_harbor_attest`, which returns `Ok` only after `session.save()?` at `edit/persist.rs:1213`. `build_snapshot` takes `&Session` (`unlock.rs:171`) and mutates nothing in between.
- **§0.3's two chains are exactly as described.** `close_all_mutation_surfaces` clears 44 fields (`:720-763`); `handle_key` dispatches 47 (`:145-383`); the difference is precisely `{tax_inputs_form, bulk_income_flow, bulk_income_modal}`. Chain A: `handle_bulk_income_modal_key`'s `Err` arm nulls only the modal (`:8697`) before `on_persist_error`, and `open_bulk_income_modal` re-opens at `:8640` with no check. Chain B: `open_pseudo_approve_flow` (`:8185-8198`) has no check, and `kat_rollback_failed_latch_refuses_all_openers` (`:21381-21414`) loops exactly `['p','c','o','r','f','v','S','d','a']`.
- **D-7's exemption is genuinely safe where it applies.** `execute_defensive_export` re-projects before planning and refuses on its own failure (`:4633-4640`), and `apply_export` (`chokepoint/mod.rs:761-778`) takes `&Session` and appends nothing — so a successful export cannot re-stale the image it just installed, and the packet, the dashboard and the year set all come from one projection.
- **The dashboard surface is inert while armed** apart from the export: `handle_defensive_dashboard_key` (`defensive_dashboard.rs:84-134`) only moves the cursor and names four intents, all of which the design gates or exempts.
- **The hoisted-status site is singular and correctly described.** `commit_tax_inputs` binds `let status: Option<String> = match outcome` at `:1332` and assigns unconditionally at `:1411`; nulls the form at `:1340` before the tail, so `close_all`'s flush has nothing to lose there.
