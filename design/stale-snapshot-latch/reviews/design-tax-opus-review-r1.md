## VERDICT

`NOT GREEN (2C/4I)` — the design's load-bearing tax assumption (§0.1/§4, "no writer re-derives a filed number from `app.snapshot`") is false for promote and for every bulk path, and §2.6 mandates copy that asserts a correctness the failed re-projection makes unknowable.

## Findings

**1. [Critical] §0.1's and §4's central tax claim is false — `promote_flow_confirm` never re-plans, and no confirm tail plans "against `session`"; the filed payload *and* the §6664(c) `shown_terms` record are built from `app.snapshot`.**
`promote_flow_confirm` (`main.rs:4450-4454`, `:4468-4480`) *extracts* the already-computed `PromotePlan` out of `PromoteFlowStep::Consent` — its own doc says "computed once at `promote_flow_review`'s PartII→Consent transition — **never recomputed here**". That plan is built at `main.rs:4367-4376` from `&snap.events, &snap.prices, &cfg`, and `plan_promote` (`chokepoint/mod.rs:406-419`) writes `Acknowledgment { shown_terms: terms.clone(), … }` *into the payload*; `apply_promote` (`:457-481`) reloads events only for `would_conflict` and appends `plan.payload` **verbatim** at `:478`. `declare_flow_confirm` does re-plan (`main.rs:4178-4188`) but off `snap`, not `session`. The bulk paths are worse: `open_bulk_classify_income_flow` (`main.rs:8352-8409`) enumerates candidates from `snap.events`/`snap.state.blockers`, `open_bulk_income_modal` (`:8612-8627`) freezes each row's `fmv` into the payload, and the confirm Enter (`:8657-8672`) merely clones them. **Filing scenario:** any of the 27 Err arms fires; the filer ignores the status (nothing stops them today), presses `w` → promote → the recorded §6664(c) `shown_terms` and the `promote_prior_year_advisory` year set (which prior years need an amended return) are derived from the pre-write ledger image and are permanent on disk; via `I` a bulk batch writes income `fmv` dollar amounts (Sch 1 income *and* the acquired lot's basis for a later 8949) frozen from that same stale image. This is not "a SHOWN readout that lags disk". The concrete design defect that follows: §2.2 **moves** the three plan-deriving sites (`:4116-4124`, `:4320-4327`, `:4368-4375`) into `editor.rs`, where they regain raw field access and therefore bypass `live_snapshot()` — exempting exactly the sites that produce the filed payload from D-5's "construction, not convention". That armed ⇒ flows-closed makes them unreachable is a *two-mechanism convention*, and D-6 exists because that very function was wrong. **Fix:** restate §0.1/§4 (this IS a filing-correctness latch); require the three migrated methods to consult the latch and refuse; add §3 KATs that no promote plan and no bulk-income payload can be constructed while armed.

**2. [Critical] §2.6's mandatory fact #1 — "the save landed and is correct" — is an assertion the design cannot make, and it directly contradicts why 13 of the 27 tails derive their status from the rebuilt snapshot.**
Those derivers exist to tell the filer the write did **not** achieve its intent: `derive_void_status` `main.rs:4714-4721` — *"Void saved, but DecisionConflict fired — the target decision remains in force"* (its doc at `:4700-4702` insists "Status must NOT start with 'Voided'"); `derive_attest_status` `:7073-7078` — *"ATTEST FAILED: allocation fires SafeHarborUnconservable … repair via CLI"*; `derive_select_lots_status` `:5757-5773` — *"…BUT it broke your safe-harbor allocation's conservation … the allocation is now inert and the year will not compute."* On the Err arm none of that is computable, because computing it *is* the re-projection that failed. Shipped copy says only "Saved but re-projection failed — restart to refresh" and asserts nothing; this design **adds** the correctness claim. **Filing scenario:** a filer voids an in-force SafeHarborAllocation decision, the append lands, re-projection fails, they read "the save landed and is correct", quit, and file as though the decision were voided — it is still in force. **Fix:** fact #1 becomes "the write reached disk; whether it had the intended effect could NOT be verified, because the ledger would not re-project" plus a named out-of-band check (`btctax verify` / `report`).

**3. [Important] "Quit and reopen" — the design's only remedy (D-4, §2.6 fact 3) — routes through the same `build_snapshot` that just failed, and can lock the filer out of both TUIs.**
`open_session` (`crates/btctax-tui/src/unlock.rs:129-136`) calls `build_snapshot` and on `Err` returns `SessionOpenOutcome::Error("failed to load vault data: {e}")` — the unlock screen, no editor; `attempt_open` (`:150-164`) does the same for the read-only viewer. Several failure arms are deterministic in on-disk content, so the restart fails identically: `load_all(conn)` / `session.config()` (`session.rs:619-627`), `tax_profile::years` / `return_inputs::years` (`session.rs:547-550`), and `LayeredPrices::load_with_cache` on a corrupt price cache (`unlock.rs:203-205`). **Filing scenario:** a week before a deadline the filer is told to restart, the restart bounces on the unlock screen, and with D-4's zero clear sites there is no in-app path back to their 8949/8275 packet. **Fix:** §2.6 must name a second-line remedy that does not go through `build_snapshot` (the CLI export/verify path), and the design should say what the filer does when the reopen fails.

**4. [Important] §2.7's "the stuck flag it warns about cannot arise" is a non-sequitur; the design reintroduces exactly the failure the shipped rationale rejects a latch for — and then deletes that rationale.**
`main.rs:4606-4610` records: the re-project-first design "cannot produce the latch's own failure mode (a stuck flag refusing a perfectly valid export)". §2.7's ordering argument (gate at `:4616` precedes the assignment at `:4630`) only prevents a *lying marker*; it says nothing about a **transient** `build_snapshot` failure — the price-cache/IO arm at `unlock.rs:203-205`. After one such blip the latch is armed forever, and the generalized `residue_latch_status()` at `:4616` refuses an export whose own re-projection at `:4626-4640` would now succeed. That is the deleted warning, verbatim, with a filing cost: no amended-year packets. `execute_defensive_export` is the one action that is correct-by-construction under staleness — it re-projects first and refuses on its own failure. **Fix:** carve it out of the generalized gate (keeping the marker), or state plainly that the owner accepts overriding `:4606-4610` and why.

**5. [Important] D-6's unconditional `self.tax_inputs_form = None` reverses a shipped "never drop work" invariant, and the guard §2.4.2 proposes structurally cannot see the write path that motivates it.**
`tax_inputs_flush_left_unsaved` (`main.rs:993-997`) plus the `Esc` and `q` arms (`:1100-1104`, `:1108-1125`) *refuse to close* the tax-inputs form when the last draft flush failed, because the in-memory `working` `ReturnInputs` — the year's entire 1040 input set — exists only in RAM ("closing then would silently drop them"). `close_all_mutation_surfaces` nulls unconditionally, and this design widens its caller set from one (`on_persist_error`'s `ResidueLive`) to twenty-eight. Separately, `flush_tax_inputs_draft` is a real disk write (`main.rs:973`) fired from the **idle tick at `main.rs:10658`, outside `handle_key` entirely** — so §2.4.2's "handle_key dispatch set ⊆ close_all clear set" guard can never cover this class, and D-1's "no in-editor action will save" is unmechanized against it. **Fix:** `close_all_mutation_surfaces` must flush first and escalate the copy when `tax_inputs_flush_left_unsaved`, never drop silently; and §3's mechanized guard must enumerate non-key write paths (idle tick, `q`) too.

**6. [Important] The §2.5 block-title marker is clippable on the one screen where the stale filed numbers are read, so D-2's "render the stale image, **marked**" is not met.**
Browse's title is `format!(" btctax-tui-edit [EDITOR] — {} ", app.vault_path.display())` (`draw_edit.rs:201-204`) — variable-length, and ratatui 0.29 clips a block title to the border width without wrapping. Goldens run at 80 columns (`draw_edit.rs:5977`, `:6205`); with a realistic vault path the title is already near that, so an appended marker disappears and the Tax/Forms tabs render pre-write numbers with **no** indication (recall `main.rs:494` clears the status, so the title is the only survivor). The tree's own precedent for this hazard class is loud and layout-shifting: the PSEUDO banner (`draw_edit.rs:213-224`), a full reversed-red row reading "FICTIONAL placeholders — DO NOT FILE". §2.5's reasons for rejecting a row are golden-churn cost; its one tax reason (PM-11 no-scrolling) applies only to flow surfaces, all of which are closed while armed. **Fix:** at minimum prepend the marker so the *path* truncates, plus a KAT rendering Browse at 80 cols with a long vault path asserting the marker survives; better, revisit the row for Browse specifically.

**7. [Minor] §2.4.3 does not pin the third latch's precedence inside `residue_latch_status()`, and the two shipped latches carry opposite remedies.**
`main.rs:672-690` orders attest > rollback, pinned by `kat_residue_latch_status_precedence` (`:21359-21371`). `attest_save_failed` means the attestation was **not** saved (retry via CLI); `stale_after_write` means the write **was** saved (do not retry). Emitting the wrong one makes the filer either re-file a landed attestation or abandon an unlanded one. Only one is armable in practice, but the order should be stated and the KAT extended.

**8. [Nit] §2.6's mandatory fact #2 ("any in-progress flow entry was discarded") is false at 26 of the 27 tails, and saying it invites a redundant re-write.**
Every tail nulls its own surface above the match — `:4219` declare, `:4500` promote, `:1340` tax-inputs commit, `:7026` attest, `:8693-8694` bulk-income. Only the park tail (`:1532-1537`) leaves a surface open, and it leaves it `dirty = false` with the draft already on disk. Make the sentence conditional on something actually having been closed; §2.6's own stated risk is the filer redoing a write that already succeeded.

## Verified sound

- **The 27-member class and its line list.** My own `awk 'NR<=10706 && /build_snapshot\(/'` reproduces `559,1347,1530,1661,2241,2588,2660,2861,3197,4217,4498,4809,5130,6147,6716,7032,7303,7787,8153,8253,8679,8968,9199,9543,9747,10031,10375` exactly, and 28 `app.snapshot = Some(snap)` assignments with `:4630` the non-tail extra. §0.1's arithmetic is right.
- **§0.2's bypass chain is real.** `main.rs:8696-8698` nulls only `bulk_income_modal` on the persist-`Err` arm; `close_all_mutation_surfaces` (`:719-764`) omits `bulk_income_flow`; `:8640` re-opens the modal with no `residue_latch_status()` check. Critical-against-shipped is the correct grade.
- **§0.2's correction of review r1 is right.** `handle_tax_inputs_key` (`:1006`+) never calls `on_persist_error`, and the tax-inputs persist wrappers return `CliError`, not `PersistError` (`edit/persist.rs:119`, `:200-210`), so that route cannot arm the *shipped* latch — only the new one, via the park tail.
- **Every ledger write is atomic on disk, including bulk.** `save_or_rollback` (`edit/persist.rs:227-232`) wraps `session.save()`; `persist_bulk_classify_income` (`:764-785`) appends the whole batch then does one save, "All-or-nothing". Brief item 4's worst case — a half-applied bulk classification stranded on disk — does not exist.
- **The export year set cannot silently shrink across sessions.** `plan_export` (`chokepoint/mod.rs:699-734`) re-derives `flagged_years` from the ledger on every call and persists no "already exported" marker; `apply_export` (`:761-778`) writes per-year packets and records nothing. A year dropped from one export reappears in the next — *provided* the reopen succeeds (finding 3).
- **The §6664(c) record cannot be half-built or truncated by `close_all_mutation_surfaces`.** `Acknowledgment` is constructed whole inside `plan_promote` (`chokepoint/mod.rs:406-419`) and appended verbatim (`:478`); `promote_flow_confirm` moves it out of the flow (`main.rs:4468-4480`) and sets `promote_flow = None` (`:4500`) before any re-projection. The consent-record risk is finding 1's stale *input*, not the close.
- **§2.3's `is_some()` dashboard guard is load-bearing and correct** — `refresh_defensive_dashboard` (`:4562-4579`) constructs `DefensiveDashboardState` unconditionally after its no-snapshot early return.
- **The wizard's write entry points are all latch-gated today** — `open_defensive_filing` (`editor.rs:474`), `open_declare_flow` (`:3997`), `open_promote_flow` (`:4253`), `execute_defensive_export` (`:4616`) — so generalizing `residue_latch_status()` really does deliver the refusals for free, exactly as §2.4.3 claims.
