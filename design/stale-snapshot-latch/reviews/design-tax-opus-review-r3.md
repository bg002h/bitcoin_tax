## Findings

**1. [Important] The payload-site set is five, not four: `handle_pseudo_approve_modal_key` builds real decision `EventPayload`s from the stale `snap.events` at write time, and neither §0.1's table, §2.3 step 4's probes, nor §3.3's two gating KATs reach it.**
`main.rs:8206-8227`: the modal's Enter computes `btctax_core::pseudo_plan(&snap.events, prices, &cfg).map(|pd| pd.decision)` — `prices` is fresh from `session.prices()` (`:8216`) but **`events` is `app.snapshot`'s** — and hands the vector verbatim to `crate::edit::persist::persist_bulk_decisions` (`:8242`), which appends every payload. These are the pseudo-reconcile synthetic defaults being promoted to *real, attested* decisions; they carry the FMV/basis that becomes a lot's basis and reaches Form 8949 column (e). §0.1's flat assertion "**Four** sites build a payload or a preview from `app.snapshot`" is therefore false as a derivation, and §3.3's stated property — "no filed payload can be built while armed", which the scope cut promotes to *the* guarantee in place of the compiler — is tested only for `PromotePlan` (`:4367`) and `DeclarePlan` (`:4172`).
It is not live today: `close_all_mutation_surfaces` nulls `pseudo_approve_modal` (`main.rs:751`), the modal is the only dispatcher once open (`:229-232`), and §2.4 step 2 adds the missing opener guard at `:8185`. But that is exactly the two-mechanism convention argument the design rejects at §2.2's ★, and with the ~176-site privatization dropped there is no longer a compiler backstop to catch the next such site. An incomplete enumeration is the specific risk the scope cut created.
**Filing scenario:** the filer approves pending pseudo defaults; the batch lands; re-projection fails; the latch arms. If any future rearrangement leaves that modal alive across the arm (a nested surface, a new dispatch path, a `None`-session arm that skips `close_all` — §2.3's own `None` arm does), `pseudo_plan` re-derives synthetic acquisitions from the pre-write event list and appends decisions whose basis is computed for events that have since changed — straight into Form 8949 col (e), with no probe and no KAT to stop it.
**Fix:** add `main.rs:8206-8227` as a fifth row of §0.1's table and a fifth `stale_reason()` probe site in §2.3 step 4, and restate §3.3's gating KAT as "no **decision payload of any kind** is constructible while armed", enumerating `plan_declare`, `plan_promote` and `pseudo_plan` as the three constructors it must cover.

**2. [Important] The marker is the only notice the filer sees from the second keypress onward, and the design never says what it says; §3.9 tests that it renders, not what it claims.**
`main.rs:414` clears `app.status` on every Browse keypress and `:494` does the same on DefensiveFiling — so §2.5's carefully-specified three facts are visible for roughly one frame. Everything after that is the §2.4 marker, and §2.4 specifies it only as "a **fixed marker text** whenever armed" (a) and "a state-conditional `Constraint::Length(1)` row" (b). No string is given for either screen; §3.9's KATs assert "armed marker renders with `status == None` on both screens", i.e. presence. The durable copy is thus the one piece of copy with no specification and no content test, while the ephemeral copy got three rounds of wording review.
This matters most on Browse, which the design itself identifies as "the screen that renders Form 8949 / Schedule D figures off `snap`": `draw_edit.rs:229-271` hands `snap` straight to `btctax_tui::tabs::{holdings,disposals,income,forms}::render`. And the Browse marker is one row — at 80 columns that is ~78 characters, so it cannot carry facts 1-3 and must be authored deliberately.
**Filing scenario:** the filer voids a decision from Browse (tail `main.rs:4809`); the append lands; re-projection fails; the latch arms. They press any key — the three facts vanish. They Tab to Forms and transcribe Form 8949 col (d)/(e) and the Schedule D totals, which are the **pre-void** figures, under a one-line marker that (as specified) may say nothing more than that a re-projection failed. Nothing on that screen tells them the numbers they are copying predate the write they just made.
**Fix:** pin the exact marker strings in §2.4 — the Browse row must, within ~78 columns, name the consequence rather than the cause (the figures on this screen predate your last write; do not file from them), and the DefensiveFiling notice its longer form — and add a **content** KAT for both (assert the substring), not only a render KAT. The PSEUDO banner (`draw_edit.rs:214-217`, "FICTIONAL placeholders — DO NOT FILE") is the in-repo precedent for the register required.

**3. [Important] D-7's export route is unreachable from the pseudo-approve tail, because `open_defensive_filing`'s DFW-D6 gate evaluates `pseudo_active()` on the stale image — and §2.5's "D-7 is the mitigation and is now genuinely reachable" is false there.**
`LedgerState::pseudo_active()` is `pseudo_synthetic_count > 0` (`btctax-core/src/state.rs:309-311`), so approving all pending defaults flips it to false. `open_defensive_filing` refuses on `snap.state.pseudo_active()` at `editor.rs:481-490` **before** it builds the view at `:503-510` — and under D-7 that function keeps the *original* latch, so the stale snapshot is what it reads.
**Filing scenario:** the filer presses `P`, approves every pseudo default; `persist_bulk_decisions` succeeds (`main.rs:8242`); `build_snapshot` fails at `:8253` (e.g. `LayeredPrices::load_with_cache`, `unlock.rs:203-205` — deterministic in on-disk content); the latch arms. The stale image still has `pseudo_synthetic_count > 0`, so `w` refuses with the DFW-D6 text that tells them to press **`P` to approve here** — and `P` now refuses too (combined latch, §2.4 step 2). Browse simultaneously keeps shouting the stale "PSEUDO-RECONCILE MODE ACTIVE … DO NOT FILE. Export blocked." banner (`draw_edit.rs:178-182`) over a ledger that is no longer pseudo. They quit as fact 3 instructs, `open_session` bounces on the same deterministic arm (`unlock.rs:129-136`), and they have no in-app route to the packet — the exact lockout D-7 exists to mitigate, on the one tail where the stale image is *guaranteed* to differ in a gate-relevant way. (The converse direction — a void that re-activates pseudo — lets the dashboard open over an image DFW-D6 exists to refuse; that one is bounded, because `x` re-projects and `plan_export`'s DFW-D11 refusal then fires on the fresh image and Declare/Promote take the combined latch. Misleading read-only view, not a wrong number.)
**Fix:** while `stale_after_write` is armed, `open_defensive_filing` must skip the DFW-D6 refusal rather than evaluate it against an image it knows is stale — safe, because the dashboard is read-only and marked, `DashboardIntent::Declare`/`Promote` take the combined latch, and `plan_export` re-derives pseudo-activity from the freshly rebuilt snapshot at `main.rs:4626-4665` and refuses there. Add a KAT: arm from the pseudo-approve tail, assert `w` opens and `x` completes. If the owner prefers to keep the gate, then §2.5 must retract "genuinely reachable", and that tail's arm copy must carry the CLI second line.

**Minor 1 — D-4's clear has no named executor at the site that performs the only clearing re-projection.** §2.3 puts the clear in `apply_reprojection`'s `Some(Ok(..))` arm, but `execute_defensive_export` does not call `apply_reprojection`: it rebuilds inline (`main.rs:4626`) and installs at `:4630`, and §3.6's tail-class guard deliberately keeps that as a second, separate `build_snapshot` entry. ## Verified sound

- **The scope cut does not reopen the surface-based path, and I re-derived the number rather than taking §0.3's word.** Extracting `handle_key`'s `app.X.is_some()` dispatch set (`main.rs:126-386`) and `close_all_mutation_surfaces`' `self.X = None` set (`:719-764`) gives **47** and **44**, with the difference exactly `{tax_inputs_form, bulk_income_flow, bulk_income_modal}` and *nothing* in the reverse direction. Once §2.3 step 1 adds those three, every keyboard-dispatched mutation surface dies at arming time, `defensive_dashboard` (dispatched via `as_mut()` at `:501`, deliberately excluded) has both of its write intents on the combined latch, and the explicit `stale_reason()` probes at the four named sites become genuine belt-and-braces rather than the primary mechanism. That is the honest characterisation the design should carry: the property is held by close_all + the 27 opener guards + §3.5(a)'s mechanized subset guard, and the §3.3 KATs pin the last line of defence at the sites the compiler was going to cover.
- **D-4's clear is safe: nothing mutates the vault between the clear and the next promote.** `persist_defensive_export` takes `&Session` (`edit/persist.rs:498-503`), `apply_export` takes `&Session` and only writes PDFs (`chokepoint/mod.rs:761-778`), and `Session::save` requires `&mut self` (`session.rs:467`) — so the export is provably read-only against the vault. The rebuilt image installed at `main.rs:4630` is therefore still current when the filer next presses `d`/`p`, and it is installed *before* `plan_export` at `:4656`, so packet, dashboard and year set come from one image.
- **No vault write escapes the 27-tail class into silent, unlatched staleness.** The three writes that reach disk without re-projecting — `form_save_draft` at `main.rs:973` (idle tick) and `:1381` (`NoTables`), and `form_discard_parked_draft` at `:1602` — all touch only `return_inputs_draft`, which is documented and confirmed **invisible to `resolve.rs`** (`input_form_store.rs:1-2`); `build_snapshot`'s profile map comes from `resolve_all_screened`, which enumerates `tax_profile::years` + `return_inputs::years` (`session.rs:549-550`), the *committed* table. So a draft autosave cannot stale `snap.profiles`, `snap.events` or `snap.prices`, and cannot corrupt any payload.
- **Both surviving per-tail prefixes are honest claims about what landed.** `form_commit` returns `Ok(CommitOutcome::Committed)` only past `mutate_and_save` (`input_form_store.rs:320-325`), and `park_to_profile` returns `Ok(())` only past the same wrapper (`:360-364`), which rolls back on a mutate *or* a save error. So "committed {year} as {label}, but…" and "parked the full return for {year}, but…" are both true at the moment they are shown, and the one prefix that claimed an unverifiable *effect* is reworded.
- **No §6664(c) record can be lost to the new `close_all(may_save = true)`.** The promote flow can only be open when the promote tail itself arms (one-flow invariant `editor.rs:436-455` + `handle_key`'s single-surface dispatch), and that tail nulls `promote_flow` at `main.rs:4500` after `persist_promote_tranche` has already recorded the `Acknowledgment` and the Part II narrative. The park tail's flush is a no-op because `form.dirty` is false both by the `t`-toggle clean-state gate and by `:1536`; the commit tail's is a no-op because `app.tax_inputs_form = None` at `:1340` precedes the rebuild at `:1347`.
- **The select-lots and lot-pick payloads are session-derived, not snapshot-derived.** `handle_sl_list_key` builds every `LotPickFormRow` from `sess.available_lots_before(...)` (`main.rs:4921-4934`), and the only `app.snapshot` read on that path is the `unconservable_before` blocker set (`:4781-4792`), which feeds `derive_select_lots_status` — display, never the payload.
- **The four named sites' remedy loses no filer work.** Refusing at `promote_flow_review` preserves the authored Part II buffer (it lives outside `step`, `main.rs:4543-4546`); refusing at `declare_flow_confirm` leaves the flow's window/sat/wallet intact; the other two are a display preview (`:4116`) and an always-refused provenance path (`:4319`).
