# SPEC — Form 6251 (Alternative Minimum Tax—Individuals), TY2025

**Status:** DRAFT r1 · written 2026-09-04 · branch `feat/schedule-1a-ty2025`
**Scope owner:** the TY2025 umbrella spec's **B2** cut (`design/ty2025/SPEC.md:618`).
**Predecessor:** `Form6251` as shipped for TY2024 (v0.14.0), `crates/btctax-core/src/tax/form6251.rs`.

> **One line.** TY2025 Form 6251 is a **port with exactly one structural change**: Part I's line 1
> becomes `1a`/`1b` so that Schedule 1-A's **enhanced senior deduction is added back to AMTI**. Parts
> II and III are line-for-line identical; only their constants move. Every existing TY2024 AMT
> computation ports unchanged.

## 2. Part I's restructure — what 1a/1b actually computes

### 2.1 The verbatim sources

Form face (`f6251--2025.txt:18-21`):

> **1a** Subtract Schedule 1-A (Form 1040), line 37, from Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 14
> **b** Subtract line 1a from Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 11b (if less than zero, enter as a
> negative amount)

Instructions, per-line (`i6251--2025.txt:189-195`):

> **Line 1a.** Enter on Form 6251, line 1a, the difference between line 14 of your Form 1040, 1040-SR,
> or 1040-NR, and line 37 of your Schedule 1-A (Form 1040).
> **Line 1b.** Enter on Form 6251, line 1b, the difference between line 1a of Form 6251 and line 11b of
> your Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR. If less than zero, enter as a negative amount.

Instructions, "What's New" (`i6251--2025.txt:31-41`) — **this is the rationale, and it names the
statute**:

> **Enhanced deduction for seniors.** New legislation generally provides an enhanced deduction for
> seniors of $6,000 for taxpayers born before January 2, 1961. The deduction is reported on Schedule
> 1-A (Form 1040), line 37. The deduction is treated as a personal exemption that is added back to
> alternative minimum taxable income as an adjustment under section 56(b)(5)(D). Accordingly, line 1
> has been revised and split into two parts, line 1a and line 1b, to account for the enhanced
> deduction for seniors as an adjustment to alternative minimum taxable income. See the instructions
> for Line 1, later.

### 2.2 The arithmetic, resolved against the 2025 Form 1040 and Schedule 1-A

The 2025 Form 1040 (`f1040--2025.txt:91-103`):

- **11a** *"Subtract line 10 from line 9. This is your adjusted gross income"*
- **11b** *"Amount from line 11a (adjusted gross income)"* — a page-2 carry-forward, so **11a ≡ 11b**
- **12e** *"Standard deduction or itemized deductions (from Schedule A)"*
- **13a** *"Qualified business income deduction from Form 8995 or Form 8995-A"*
- **13b** *"Additional deductions from Schedule 1-A, line 38"*
- **14** *"Add lines 12e, 13a, and 13b"*
- **15** *"Subtract line 14 from line 11b. If zero or less, enter -0-. This is your taxable income"*

Schedule 1-A (`f1040s1a--2025.txt:108-111`):

- **37** *"Enhanced deduction for seniors. Add lines 36a and 36b"* — **Part V only**
- **38** *"Add lines 13, 21, 30, and 37"* — the total (tips + overtime + car-loan interest + senior)

Substituting:

```
1a = L14 − Sch1A_37 = 12e + 13a + (tips + overtime + carloan + senior) − senior
                    = 12e + 13a + tips + overtime + carloan
1b = 11b − 1a       = AGI − 12e − 13a − tips − overtime − carloan
                    = (unfloored taxable income) + senior
```

**Machine-verified** (script run 2026-09-04, worked household AGI 400,000 / 12e 31,500 / tips 5,000 /
overtime 3,000 / car-loan 2,000 / senior 6,000): `1a = 41,500`, `1b = 358,500`, taxable income
`352,500`, and `358,500 = 352,500 + 6,000` ✔; and `1b + tips + overtime + carloan = 368,500 =
AGI − 12e − 13a` ✔.

### 2.3 The answer to the question — TWO effects, one substantive

**(a) SUBSTANTIVE — the AMTI starting point changed.** TY2024's line 1 is the unfloored taxable
income. TY2025's line 1b is **the unfloored taxable income plus the Schedule 1-A line 37 senior
deduction**. The senior deduction is allowed for the regular tax and **disallowed for the AMT**; the
other three OBBBA deductions (tips, overtime, car-loan interest) are **allowed for both**. That
asymmetry is invisible in the form's numbering and is exactly what a compressed "closed form" would
lose — line 1a subtracts **37**, not **38**.

**(b) PRESENTATIONAL — the conditional is gone.** TY2024 branched on *"if more than zero … if line 15
is zero…"* to recover the unfloored value that line 15's floor destroys. TY2025 computes
`11b − (14 − Sch1A_37)` directly and never consults line 15, so **no branch, no floor, and no write-in
caveat** (§1.5 item 3). For a filer with **no senior deduction, TY2025 line 1b is numerically
identical to what TY2024 line 1 would have produced.**

### 2.4 Consequence for the port — the verdict this spec exists to give

> **The existing TY2024 AMT logic PORTS. It is not rewritten.**

Everything downstream of Part I consumes exactly one number — *the amount line 4 combines* — and
`Form6251` already routes it that way. The code has anticipated this: `Form6251Line1`
(`crates/btctax-core/src/tax/form6251.rs:40-80`) is already a per-year enum landed by commit
`e278c052` *"ty2025(B2): Form 6251 Part I is a per-year TYPE, not a year flag"*, with the TY2025 arm's
verbatim text already in its doc comment (`:53-59`). **What remains is to populate the TY2025 arm and
wire its inputs — not to redesign anything.**

Concretely, what must change:

| # | change | why |
|---|---|---|
| **P-1** | `Form6251Line1::Y2025 { line1a, line1b }` gains its computation and its `combined()` → `line1b` | §2.2 |
| **P-2** | `AmtParams` for TY2025 inserted into `BundledFullReturnTables::load()` | §4 — it does not exist today |
| **P-3** | line 2a reads 1040 **12e** for TY2025 | §1.2 |
| **P-4** | the senior-deduction input reaches Form 6251 | §3.2 |
| **P-5** | `cover_form6251line1`'s TY2025 arm + a per-row year (§7.3) | conformance |
| **P-6** | TY2025 `f6251.pdf` + `f6251.map.toml` filing assets | §3.3 |

## 3. The cross-form seams

### 3.1 Line 2a ↔ Schedule A line 7 — **THE SEAM HOLDS. Verified, not assumed.**

This was flagged as the highest-value possible finding. **It is not a finding: the two forms agree.**

Form 6251 TY2025 line 2a (`f6251--2025.txt:22-23`) still reads *"If filing Schedule A (Form 1040),
enter the taxes from Schedule A, line 7"*. And Schedule A **line 7 is unchanged in both text and
meaning**:

```
f1040sa--2024.txt:32:   7 Add lines 5e and 6
f1040sa--2025.txt:36:   7 Add lines 5e and 6
```

**Byte-identical instruction, identical referent** — SALT (as limited) plus other taxes. Schedule A's
restructure lands entirely **inside line 5e**, which line 7 sums without knowing about it:

| | TY2024 (`f1040sa--2024.txt:24-25`) | TY2025 (`f1040sa--2025.txt:29-32`) |
|---|---|---|
| 5e | *"Enter the smaller of line 5d or **$10,000** ($5,000 if married filing separately)"* — a bare cap, no worksheet | *"Enter the smaller of line 5d or **$40,000** ($20,000 if married filing separately). If Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11b is more than **$500,000** ($250,000 if married filing separately), or if you completed Form 2555, Form 4563, or excluded income from Puerto Rico, **see instructions**"* — resolved by the State and Local Tax Deduction Worksheet, `i1040sca--2025.txt:862` |

**So Form 6251 needs no change for the SALT restructure.** It reads line 7; line 7 means what it
always meant.

★ **But the seam is now load-bearing in a way it was not.** The add-back at line 2a is capped at
Schedule A line 5e, which rose from **$10,000 to as much as $40,000** — a **4× larger AMT add-back**
for a high-SALT itemiser. That is the OBBBA design (the SALT relief is partly clawed back through the
AMT), and it means **TY2025 pushes materially more filers into AMT than TY2024 did at the same
income.** It also raises the stakes on `FOLLOWUPS.md` §G-6d (*"no fixture vector has Schedule A line
7 > 0"*): a TY2025 vector set with a zero line 2a is not merely incomplete, it now misses the
dominant new trigger. **§7's KAT set therefore requires a Schedule-A-line-7 > 0 vector at TY2025.**

★★ **The dependency runs Schedule A → Form 6251, and only that way.** Form 6251 line 2a is a pure
read. If the other agent's Schedule A work changes line 5e's *value*, Form 6251 needs **no** edit; if
it were ever to change line 7's *definition*, this spec's §7.1 conformance quote would go red
automatically, because the quote is checked verbatim against `f1040sa--2025.txt`. **The seam is
already instrumented.** No new coupling test is required.

### 3.2 Line 1a ↔ Schedule 1-A line 37 — the new seam, and its fail-closed rule

This is the seam that **does** need building. Form 6251 line 1a requires Schedule 1-A **line 37**.
Schedule 1-A is **B3**, a separate spec and branch, and it is **not yet built**.

**The rule (fail-closed, and it is not optional):**

- A TY2025 return in which Schedule 1-A line 37 is **unknown** (never collected) must **refuse**, not
  substitute zero. A hardcoded `0` here is indistinguishable on the printed page from a filer with no
  senior deduction, and it **understates AMTI by up to $12,000 (MFJ, two spouses × $6,000)** — an
  understatement of tax, which is the blocking class.
- A TY2025 return in which Schedule 1-A line 37 is **known to be zero** (the filer is not a senior, or
  is phased out) computes normally, and **line 1b then equals what TY2024's line 1 would have given**
  (§2.3). This is the common case and it must not refuse.

★ This is the answered-ness invariant verbatim: *"blank because the inputs say so"* is correct;
*"blank because nothing populated it"* is the defect, and the two are identical on the page. The
distinction must be **structural** — `Option`-shaped and refusing on `None` — never a default.

**Ordering.** Form 6251 TY2025 therefore **depends on B3** for a complete return. It does **not**
depend on B3 to be *specified*, which is why this document can be written now. §6's refusal `FR-6251-1`
is what makes the intermediate state safe.

### 3.3 Filing assets — absent

`crates/btctax-forms/forms/2025/` contains `f1040`, `f8283`, `f8949`, `schedule_d`, `schedule_se`
only. **There is no `2025/f6251.pdf` and no `2025/f6251.map.toml`.** `design/forms/2025/f6251--2025.pdf`
is archived for *reading*, not for *filling*. Emission of a TY2025 Form 6251 is therefore impossible
today and must refuse (§6, `FR-6251-3`) rather than fall back to the TY2024 map — a 2024 map applied to
the 2025 AcroForm would silently misplace 1a/1b.

## 5. Oracle limits carried forward — and what changes

### 5.1 The TY2024 picture (established, `FOLLOWUPS.md` §G-6 / `CONTINUITY_TY2025.md:74-95`)

For MFS, §55(d)(3) makes the zero-exemption threshold and the line-4 kicker start **the same amount**
— definitional, not a coincidence of one year. At that point both TY2024 oracles fail:

| engine | failure at the MFS kicker, TY2024 |
|---|---|
| OpenTaxSolver 2024 | implements the add-back with **stale 2023** constants — `taxsolve_US_1040_2024.c:270-275` |
| Tax-Calculator 6.7.2 | does not model the add-back at all; no MFS branch in its `c62100` block |

Vectors **V23/V24/V25** therefore owe AMT with **zero witnesses**.

### 5.2 ★ TY2025 — the collision PERSISTS, but the blindness does NOT

**The collision persists**, and §4.3 proves it for 2025's own constants: MFS zero-exemption
**900,350** = kicker start **900,350**. It is structural; no year will separate them.

**The blindness does not persist.** OTS 2024's defect was a stale-constant bug in one year's solver,
not blindness to the rule. **OpenTaxSolver 2025 implements it correctly**, smoke-tested end to end on
a deliberately-above-the-kicker MFS household (`CONTINUITY_TY2025.md:120-133`):

```
MFS, wages 260,000, LTCG 700,000, cash gift 25,000  ->  taxable income 935,000
OTS 2025 line 4  = 943,662.50 == 935,000 + 0.25 × (935,000 − 900,350)   ✔ kicker applied
OTS 2025 line 5  = 0                                                     ✔ exemption zeroed
OTS 2025 line 11 = 13,571.50, and 1040 L17 = 13,571.50                   ✔ 30 lines printed
```

Tax-Calculator also covers 2025 **with both AMT defects intact**, so it remains a
*named-disqualification* witness there — which the gate rule accepts.

> **The verdict: TY2025 turns the dark MFS region from ZERO witnesses into ONE VERIFIED witness.**
> That is the whole reason `CONTINUITY_TY2025.md:74` puts TY2025 ahead of E4. **This spec confirms the
> premise still holds** against the 2025 form and instructions as archived.

### 5.3 What the witness census must assert for TY2025

`scripts/oracle/verify_f6251.py` already carries a per-vector `year` (`_year_of`, default 2024,
`:67-73`) and a `_witness_census` keyed off it (`:293-343`). Four requirements:

1. **Both exclusion lists stay COMPUTED from the defect's mechanism, never keyed by vector name.** The
   two name-keyed lists this harness once had both went stale on the first batch of new vectors. A
   TY2025 vector set is exactly such a batch.
2. **OTS's TY2024 MFS disqualification must be YEAR-SCOPED and must NOT apply at TY2025.** It is a
   2024-solver bug that OTS 2025 fixes. Leaving it year-blind would suppress the very witness this
   pivot exists to acquire — a green run that proves nothing.
3. **taxcalc's MFS add-back omission stays disqualified at TY2025**, since the defect is
   year-independent, and its **size** must still be named so a divergence of the wrong shape is
   unexpected even on an expected-to-diverge vector.
4. **The census must FAIL the run if any filing status loses its last two-oracle AMT-owing vector**,
   and must print the fixture years present. With OTS 2025 as the second witness, the TY2025 MFS
   above-the-kicker vectors **must reach two witnesses**. If they do not, the pivot did not work and
   that is a blocking finding, not a note.

★ Note the standing limit that no oracle agreement can cure: **a value the oracles take as INPUT is
never validated by their agreement.** Schedule 1-A line 37 is handed to OTS as an input, so **two
oracles agreeing on line 1b does not validate the senior deduction itself** — only the add-back
arithmetic around it. Line 37's own correctness is B3's problem, and §7's KAT must pin it independently.

### 6.2 Non-scope, each with its refusal

Every non-scope item below is a **refusal**, not a silent zero. Wording follows the house voice

| id | condition | `RefuseReason` | exact operator-facing wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FR-6251-1** | TY2025 return, Form 6251 required, and Schedule 1-A line 37 was **never collected** | `Sch1ALine37Unanswered` | `tax year 2025 cannot be computed from its full-return inputs: Form 6251 line 1a subtracts Schedule 1-A line 37 (the enhanced deduction for seniors), which was never collected. ★ **FR-6251-1 must fail closed on `None`, never on a sentinel.** A `0` default here is the exact
shape of the shipped-and-laundered ISO-exercise defect: correct on the page, wrong in fact,
undetectable by any test that checks the *value*.

★ **FR-6251-3 is deliberately NOT a whole-return refusal.** A filer whose AMT is $0 needs no Form 6251
attached at all ("Who Must File" is not met), and their printed forms are byte-identical with or
without the asset. Refusing the whole return for a missing asset nobody needs would be a **false
refusal** — the understatement-mirror this repo has been bitten by. FR-6251-3 fires only on the
emission path, and only when AMT > 0.

- **Line 2r's `section 174A(a)` renumbering** (§1.5 item 4). Line 2r is not modelled in either year;

## 10. Open questions for review

1. **Does `Form6251Line1::Y2025` carry `line1a` as a stored field or derive it?** The form prints it,
   so it is a box on the page and must be stored to be emitted; but it is fully determined by 1040 L14
   and Sch 1-A L37. **Recommendation: store it** — a printed box is testimony and must round-trip
   through E4's read-back. The plan decides.
2. **`Coverage::line` hardcodes `year: DEFAULT_ROW_YEAR` ("2024")** at `line_coverage.rs:148` and
   `:170`, while the struct field's own doc comment (`:102-106`) says *"Per-row, not a module
   constant … A TY2025+ form sets its own."* **There is no constructor that lets a row set its year.**
   A TY2025 row is therefore inexpressible through the public API today (a direct struct literal works
   — the fields are `pub` — but nothing uses it). ★ This is the doc-comment-claims-a-capability shape;
   it needs a `line_in_year` constructor before P-5. **Flagged, not fixed: it is B2 plumbing and may
   belong to whoever lands `cover_form6251line1`'s TY2025 arm.**
3. **Should FR-6251-1 refuse, or forfeit the senior deduction?** Forfeiting (treating line 37 as its
   *maximum*, $6,000/person) **overstates** AMTI and is lawful-but-adverse; refusing is this repo's
   default. **Recommendation: refuse.** A silent overstatement is still a wrong figure on sworn
   testimony, and the filer can simply answer the question.
